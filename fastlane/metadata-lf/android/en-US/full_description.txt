3 Lakes Light Fleet is the official driver app for 3 Lakes Logistics light fleet and NEMT contractors.

FEATURES:
• Trip Board — View and accept available gig trips, courier runs, and NEMT rides
• Current Trip — Full trip details with one-tap navigation
• Earnings Dashboard — Track daily, weekly, and lifetime earnings in real time
• Instant Pay — Request same-day payout to your bank account via Stripe
• Compliance Status — View your active compliance items and document deadlines
• Document Upload — Submit required documents directly from your phone
• Driver Profile — Personal info, vehicle details, and performance score
• Emergency Contacts — One-tap access to dispatch and roadside assistance
• Offline Support — App remains functional without a live connection

Built for gig drivers, NEMT contractors, and courier specialists. Requires an active driver account with 3 Lakes Logistics Light Fleet.
