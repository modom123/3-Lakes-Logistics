RECITALS

WHEREAS, the Company is engaged in the business of freight brokerage, third-party logistics (3PL), transportation dispatch, owner-operator management, carrier relationship management, shipper account management, and related services operating in California and throughout the United States;

WHEREAS, in connection with the Covered Party's role with the Company, the Covered Party will receive access to valuable confidential information, including but not limited to carrier relationships, customer lists, rate data, operational processes, and trade secrets;

WHEREAS, the Company has invested substantial resources in developing its carrier network, customer base, proprietary technology, and operational methods, all of which constitute legitimate business interests worthy of protection;

WHEREAS, the Company desires to protect these legitimate business interests through reasonable restrictions, and the Covered Party has received or will receive adequate consideration in connection with this Agreement;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the Covered Party's employment or engagement with the Company, access to Confidential Information, the compensation and benefits provided, and other good and valuable consideration (see Section 5), the Parties agree as follows:

SECTION 1 — DEFINITIONS

1.1 Definitions

"Carrier" means any trucking company, owner-operator, fleet, or transportation provider that: (a) has been introduced to the Covered Party through the Company; (b) has hauled a load arranged through the Company's brokerage operations; or (c) appears in the Company's carrier database or TMS system.

"Customer" or "Shipper" means any person, company, or organization that: (a) has tendered freight to the Company or requested logistics services from the Company; (b) has been solicited by the Covered Party on behalf of the Company; (c) is in active negotiations or discussions with the Company regarding freight services; or (d) has been a customer of the Company at any time during the twelve (12) months preceding the Covered Party's separation from the Company.

"Restricted Period" means the period beginning on the date the Covered Party's employment or engagement with the Company terminates (for any reason) and ending as specified in the applicable section below.

"Competing Business" means any person, company, entity, or enterprise engaged in freight brokerage, third-party logistics (3PL), dispatch services, owner-operator management, or carrier relations management that directly competes with the Company's services within the Restricted Territory.

"Restricted Territory" means: (a) for the non-compete provisions, a radius of fifty (50) miles from the Covered Party's primary work location at the time of separation; and (b) for non-solicitation provisions, throughout the United States, as the Company operates nationally.

"Confidential Information" has the meaning set forth in the Company's Non-Disclosure Agreement, which is incorporated herein by reference.

SECTION 2 — NON-SOLICITATION OF CARRIERS

2.1 Twelve-Month Carrier Non-Solicitation

During the Covered Party's engagement with the Company and for a period of twelve (12) months following the termination of such engagement for any reason (the "Carrier Non-Solicitation Period"), the Covered Party shall not, directly or indirectly:

  • Solicit, induce, recruit, or encourage any Carrier to reduce, divert, terminate, or modify its business relationship with the Company;
  • Arrange, broker, dispatch, or otherwise facilitate any freight movement using any Carrier in a manner that bypasses or competes with the Company, where the Covered Party first learned of or established a relationship with such Carrier through the Company;
  • Disclose Carrier contact information, rate agreements, performance data, or other Confidential Information to any third party for the purpose of establishing a competing carrier relationship;
  • Accept or solicit business from any Carrier on behalf of a Competing Business where the Covered Party's ability to solicit or serve such Carrier is materially enabled by Confidential Information or relationships developed during the Covered Party's engagement with the Company.

2.2 Scope of Carrier Protection

This carrier non-solicitation applies to all Carriers with whom the Covered Party had direct contact or about whom the Covered Party received Confidential Information during the Covered Party's engagement with the Company, including Carriers identified through the Company's DAT load board access, proprietary carrier database, or internal referrals.

SECTION 3 — NON-SOLICITATION OF CUSTOMERS AND SHIPPERS

3.1 Twelve-Month Customer Non-Solicitation

During the Covered Party's engagement with the Company and for a period of twelve (12) months following the termination of such engagement for any reason (the "Customer Non-Solicitation Period"), the Covered Party shall not, directly or indirectly:

  • Solicit, contact, or accept business from any Customer or Shipper for the purpose of providing freight brokerage, dispatch, or logistics services that compete with the Company's services;
  • Induce, encourage, or assist any Customer or Shipper to reduce, divert, or terminate its business relationship with the Company;
  • Use or disclose Confidential Information, including Customer contact information, shipping volumes, lane preferences, or rate data, for the purpose of soliciting or competing for the business of any Customer or Shipper;
  • Assist any third party in soliciting the business of any Customer or Shipper with whom the Covered Party had contact during the Covered Party's engagement with the Company.

3.2 Passive Contact Exception

The Customer non-solicitation in Section 3.1 shall not prohibit the Covered Party from responding to an unsolicited inquiry from a former Customer, provided that: (a) the Covered Party did not initiate the contact; (b) the Covered Party does not use any Confidential Information in responding to or servicing such inquiry; and (c) the Covered Party promptly informs the Company of such unsolicited contact.

SECTION 4 — NON-SOLICITATION OF EMPLOYEES AND CONTRACTORS

4.1 Twelve-Month Employee/Contractor Non-Solicitation

During the Covered Party's engagement with the Company and for a period of twelve (12) months following the termination of such engagement for any reason, the Covered Party shall not, directly or indirectly:

  • Solicit, recruit, hire, or attempt to hire any employee, officer, manager, or independent contractor of the Company;
  • Encourage, induce, or assist any employee or contractor of the Company to terminate their relationship with the Company;
  • Provide references or other assistance to any person or entity seeking to recruit Company employees or contractors for competing positions;
  • Act as a conduit through which a Competing Business solicits Company employees or contractors.

SECTION 5 — LIMITED NON-COMPETE (INDEPENDENT CONTRACTORS ONLY)

IMPORTANT — CALIFORNIA LAW: Pursuant to California Business and Professions Code § 16600, non-compete agreements with employees are generally void and unenforceable in California. Section 5 of this Agreement applies ONLY to independent contractors and is intended to be narrowly tailored and ancillary to the protection of trade secrets. If the Covered Party is classified as an employee, Section 5 shall be deemed void and severed from this Agreement without affecting any other provision.

5.1 Six-Month Non-Compete for Independent Contractors

If and only if the Covered Party is an independent contractor (not an employee), then for a period of six (6) months following the termination of the Covered Party's engagement with the Company for any reason (the "Non-Compete Period"), the Covered Party shall not, within the Restricted Territory (fifty (50) mile radius of the Covered Party's primary work location):

  • Own, operate, manage, control, or serve as an officer, director, employee, partner, consultant, or contractor of any Competing Business;
  • Perform freight dispatch services, freight brokerage services, or owner-operator management services on behalf of any Competing Business within the Restricted Territory;
  • Establish, fund, or participate in the establishment of any Competing Business within the Restricted Territory.

5.2 Trade Secret Nexus

The non-compete restriction in Section 5.1 is entered into for the purpose of protecting the Company's trade secrets and Confidential Information and is ancillary to a lawful agreement as provided under applicable California authority governing trade secret protection. The Covered Party acknowledges that, in performing the Services for the Company, the Covered Party will receive and have access to trade secrets of the Company that cannot adequately be protected through less restrictive means.

5.3 Geographic and Duration Reasonableness

The Parties acknowledge and agree that: (a) the fifty (50) mile geographic restriction is reasonable given the local and regional nature of the Company's primary dispatch operations; (b) the six (6) month duration is the minimum period reasonably necessary to protect the Company's trade secrets; and (c) if any court determines this restriction to be broader than necessary, the Parties authorize such court to modify the restriction to the minimum extent necessary to make it enforceable.

  • Employment or engagement with the Company and all compensation and benefits paid or provided thereunder;
  • Training, professional development, and access to the Company's established carrier network, customer relationships, and industry contacts;
  • The following additional specific consideration (if applicable): _______________________________

6.2 New Employment/Engagement

If this Agreement is signed in connection with the commencement of a new employment or engagement relationship (rather than during an existing relationship), the Covered Party acknowledges that the offer of employment or engagement itself constitutes adequate consideration for this Agreement.

SECTION 7 — EXCEPTIONS AND CARVE-OUTS

7.1 General Employment Exception (Employees)

For Covered Parties who are employees, the restrictions in Sections 2, 3, and 4 shall be interpreted and applied in a manner consistent with California law. Nothing in this Agreement shall prevent an employee from: (a) seeking or accepting employment with any employer; (b) using general industry knowledge, skills, and experience that do not constitute the Company's Confidential Information or trade secrets; or (c) engaging in lawful activity protected by California Labor Code § 96(k) or other applicable law.

7.2 Public Information Exception

The restrictions herein shall not apply to any information or relationships that are generally available to the public and not derived from the Company's Confidential Information.

7.3 Prior Relationships

The Carrier and Customer non-solicitation provisions shall not apply to any Carrier or Customer with whom the Covered Party had a pre-existing, documented business relationship before the commencement of the Covered Party's engagement with the Company, provided such relationship is disclosed in writing to the Company within thirty (30) days of the Effective Date of this Agreement. The Covered Party shall attach to this Agreement a list of all such pre-existing relationships as Exhibit A.

7.4 Scope Limitation — Reasonable Interpretation

All restrictions herein shall be interpreted as narrowly as necessary to be enforceable under applicable California law while still achieving the legitimate business purpose of protecting the Company's Confidential Information and trade secrets.

SECTION 8 — REMEDIES

8.1 Injunctive Relief

The Covered Party acknowledges that any breach or threatened breach of this Agreement would cause irreparable harm to the Company for which monetary damages would be inadequate, and therefore the Company shall be entitled to seek injunctive relief, specific performance, and other equitable remedies without the necessity of posting bond or proving actual damages.

8.2 Tolling

In the event the Covered Party breaches any of the non-solicitation provisions herein, the Restricted Period applicable to such provision shall be tolled and shall not run during the period of breach, so that the Company receives the full benefit of the agreed restriction.

8.3 Monetary Damages and Attorneys' Fees

The Company shall also be entitled to recover monetary damages for any breach of this Agreement, including lost profits, disgorgement of wrongfully obtained profits, costs of investigation, and reasonable attorneys' fees.

SECTION 9 — GENERAL PROVISIONS

9.1 Governing Law

This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of California, without regard to conflict of laws principles. The Parties agree that all disputes shall be resolved exclusively in state or federal courts located in California.

9.2 Severability and Blue-Penciling

If any provision of this Agreement is found to be invalid or unenforceable, the Parties authorize the court to modify such provision to the minimum extent necessary to make it enforceable rather than voiding it in its entirety. The invalidity of any provision shall not affect the validity of any other provision.

9.3 Entire Agreement

This Agreement, together with the Company's NDA and any applicable employment or contractor agreement, constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties with respect to the subject matter of restrictive covenants and supersedes all prior agreements on that subject.

9.4 Amendment

This Agreement may only be amended by a written instrument signed by both Parties.

9.5 Counterparts and Electronic Signatures

This Agreement may be executed in counterparts. Electronic signatures are valid and binding.
