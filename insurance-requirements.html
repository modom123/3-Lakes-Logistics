Insurance Minimum Requirements

Effective Date: June 1, 2026  |  3 Lakes Logistics LLC  |  State of California

MANDATORY COMPLIANCE. All carriers, owner-operators, and subcontractors performing services for 3 Lakes Logistics LLC must meet and maintain the insurance minimums stated herein at all times. Failure to maintain required insurance is grounds for immediate termination of any dispatch or service agreement.

1. Required Insurance Coverage

1.1 Commercial Auto Liability

Vehicle TypeMinimum Coverage
Semi-Truck / Tractor-Trailer (GVWR > 26,000 lbs)$1,000,000 combined single limit
Straight Truck / Box Truck (GVWR 10,001–26,000 lbs)$750,000 combined single limit
Cargo Van / Sprinter (GVWR ≤ 10,000 lbs)$300,000 combined single limit
NEMT / Passenger Transport$1,500,000 combined single limit
Hazmat Transport$5,000,000 combined single limit

1.2 Motor Truck Cargo (Freight) Insurance

Commodity TypeMinimum Coverage
General Freight$100,000 per occurrence
High-Value / Electronics$250,000 per occurrence
Temperature-Controlled / Perishables$150,000 per occurrence
NEMT / Medical Supplies$50,000 per occurrence

1.3 General Liability

All carriers must maintain Commercial General Liability insurance with minimum limits of $1,000,000 per occurrence and $2,000,000 aggregate.

1.4 Workers' Compensation / Occupational Accident

All carriers with employees must maintain statutory Workers' Compensation insurance. Owner-Operators with no employees must maintain Occupational Accident insurance with minimum coverage of $500,000 accidental death/dismemberment and $250,000 medical benefits.

2. Certificate of Insurance Requirements

All certificates of insurance must: (a) name "3 Lakes Logistics LLC" as an additional insured; (b) include a 30-day notice of cancellation clause; (c) list the policy effective and expiration dates; (d) be issued by an insurance company with an AM Best rating of A- or better; (e) be provided before the first load dispatch and renewed annually.

3. FMCSA Filing Requirements

Carriers operating under their own authority must maintain minimum FMCSA-required insurance filings (Form BMC-91 or BMC-91X) as applicable. 3 Lakes Logistics LLC will verify insurance status through the FMCSA SAFER system prior to dispatch.

4. Non-Compliant Carriers

Carriers whose insurance lapses, is cancelled, or falls below minimum requirements will be immediately removed from active dispatch status until compliance is restored. 3 Lakes Logistics LLC is not liable for any claims arising from non-compliant carrier operations.

5. Acknowledgment

By signing below, Carrier confirms that it currently meets all insurance requirements stated herein, agrees to maintain such coverage, and will immediately notify 3 Lakes Logistics LLC of any changes to its insurance status.
