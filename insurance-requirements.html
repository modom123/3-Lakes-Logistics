Carrier Insurance Minimum Requirements Letter

Effective Date: June 1, 2026  |  3 Lakes Logistics LLC  |  State of California

NOTICE TO ALL CARRIERS: 3 Lakes Logistics LLC requires all carriers hauling freight arranged or brokered by 3LL to maintain the insurance coverages described in this document at all times. Failure to maintain required insurance or to provide current certificates of insurance will result in immediate suspension from 3LL's approved carrier list and the withholding of pending settlements until compliance is established.

INTRODUCTION

3 Lakes Logistics LLC ("3LL") is committed to protecting the interests of its shipper clients, its operations, and the general public. As a condition of doing business with 3LL — whether as a motor carrier, owner-operator, or subhauler — every carrier must maintain the insurance coverages specified in this document. These requirements reflect the minimum thresholds necessary to ensure that cargo is protected, liability exposures are covered, and all parties to the transportation transaction are adequately protected in the event of a loss.

This document serves as 3LL's official Carrier Insurance Minimum Requirements Letter. All carriers accepting loads from 3LL agree, by their acceptance of a Rate Confirmation or their execution of a Broker-Carrier Agreement with 3LL, to comply with all requirements set forth herein.

1.2 Commercial Auto Liability — Detail

Minimum Limit: $1,000,000 Combined Single Limit (CSL) per occurrence

This coverage must apply to all power units and trailers operated by the carrier in connection with any load tendered by 3LL. The policy must include the MCS-90 endorsement (or its equivalent) as required by FMCSA regulations for interstate motor carriers. For carriers operating vehicles with a gross vehicle weight rating (GVWR) of 10,001 lbs or more in interstate commerce, this coverage is the minimum required by federal law under 49 CFR § 387.9.

Note for Hazmat Carriers: Carriers transporting hazardous materials in quantities requiring placarding must maintain a minimum of $1,000,000 auto liability for non-bulk commodities and $5,000,000 for certain bulk hazmat commodities, as required by 49 CFR § 387.9.

1.3 Motor Truck Cargo Insurance — Detail

Minimum Limit: $100,000 per occurrence

Cargo insurance must cover loss or damage to the freight being transported. The policy must cover all commodities transported by the carrier on behalf of 3LL unless specific exclusions have been disclosed to and accepted by 3LL in writing. The cargo policy must:

  • Cover loading and unloading of cargo;
  • Not exclude any commodity type hauled for 3LL without prior written disclosure;
  • Have a deductible not to exceed $2,500 per occurrence without prior written approval from 3LL;
  • Name 3 Lakes Logistics LLC as an additional insured or loss payee, as applicable;
  • Cover the full transit from pickup to delivery without territorial restrictions that would affect coverage on the routes operated for 3LL.

1.4 Commercial General Liability — Detail

Minimum Limit: $1,000,000 per occurrence / $2,000,000 general aggregate

CGL coverage protects against bodily injury and property damage claims not covered by the auto liability policy. This coverage must apply to the carrier's operations as a whole and must not be limited in a manner that excludes coverage for the carrier's freight transportation activities. 3LL must be named as an additional insured on the CGL policy.

1.5 Workers' Compensation and Employer's Liability — Detail

Any carrier that employs drivers or other workers must maintain workers' compensation insurance as required by the laws of each state in which it employs workers. For carriers operating in California, this means compliance with California Labor Code requirements and the California workers' compensation system. Employer's liability coverage at minimum limits of $100,000 (each accident) / $500,000 (disease, policy limit) / $100,000 (disease, each employee) is required for carriers with employees.

Owner-operators who operate as independent contractors with no employees may, in lieu of workers' compensation, maintain an occupational accident policy meeting 3LL's minimum requirements. The carrier must document its owner-operator classification in writing and provide evidence of occupational accident coverage upon request.

2.2 Certificate Format

Certificates of Insurance must be submitted using the ACORD 25 form (or an equivalent form acceptable to 3LL). The certificate must clearly show:

  • The name and contact information of the insurance company and agent;
  • The insured's name as it appears on the policy;
  • The policy number;
  • The policy effective and expiration dates;
  • Coverage limits for each required coverage type;
  • 3 Lakes Logistics LLC named as an additional insured;
  • The thirty (30) day cancellation notice requirement (see Section 2.3);
  • The agent or insurance company's authorized signature or electronic equivalent.

2.3 Thirty-Day Cancellation Notice Requirement

All insurance policies maintained by the carrier for the coverages required herein must include a provision requiring the insurer to provide 3LL with at least thirty (30) days advance written notice of any cancellation, material modification, or non-renewal of coverage. In the event of cancellation for non-payment of premium, a minimum of ten (10) days notice is required. The Certificate of Insurance submitted to 3LL must reflect this notification requirement.

2.4 When Certificates Must Be Submitted

Carriers must provide a current Certificate of Insurance to 3LL:

  • Prior to being approved as a new carrier and placed on 3LL's approved carrier list;
  • Prior to picking up the first load after any interruption in the carrier's relationship with 3LL;
  • Immediately upon any change in the carrier's insurance coverage, insurer, policy limits, or policy expiration date;
  • Annually, upon each policy renewal (see Section 4); and
  • Upon 3LL's request at any time.

2.5 Certificate Submission

Certificates of Insurance should be emailed to: info@3lakeslogistics.com with the subject line "COI — [Carrier Name] — [MC#]." 3LL may also request that the certificate be faxed or submitted through a carrier compliance platform as specified by 3LL.

SECTION 3 — FMCSA VERIFICATION AND CARRIER QUALIFICATION

3.1 FMCSA Authority Verification

All carriers doing business with 3LL must hold active, unrestricted FMCSA operating authority appropriate for the type of freight they will transport. 3LL verifies carrier authority through the FMCSA's SAFER system (safer.fmcsa.dot.gov) and the Carrier411 or similar carrier monitoring services. Carriers must not have any open out-of-service orders, conditional safety ratings, or suspended authorities at the time of any load acceptance.

3.2 FMCSA Safety Rating

3LL will not dispatch loads to carriers with an FMCSA safety rating of "Unsatisfactory." Carriers with a "Conditional" safety rating are subject to additional review and may be approved on a load-by-load basis at 3LL's discretion. Carriers with no FMCSA safety rating (new authority) will be reviewed individually, including a review of the carrier's SMS (Safety Measurement System) scores and any available safety history.

3.3 Carrier Monitoring

3LL subscribes to carrier monitoring services that provide real-time alerts regarding changes in carrier authority, insurance, safety ratings, and out-of-service orders. If a carrier's status changes in a way that affects its eligibility to haul loads for 3LL, 3LL may immediately suspend dispatch of loads to that carrier pending resolution of the issue.

3.4 Carrier Packet / Onboarding Documentation

In addition to insurance certificates, new carriers must complete 3LL's Carrier Packet, which includes:

  • W-9 (or W-8BEN for non-US entities);
  • Copy of FMCSA operating authority (MC Certificate) or FMCSA printout;
  • Copy of USDOT registration;
  • Signed Broker-Carrier Agreement;
  • Current Certificate of Insurance (meeting all requirements herein);
  • ELD compliance certification or documentation;
  • Contact information for carrier dispatch, accounting, and claims departments;
  • ACH/direct deposit information (if requesting electronic payment);
  • Factoring company NOA (if applicable).

SECTION 4 — ANNUAL RENEWAL REQUIREMENTS

4.1 Annual Certificate Renewal

All insurance certificates expire. Carriers are responsible for providing 3LL with updated Certificates of Insurance upon each annual policy renewal and no later than five (5) business days before the expiration of the current certificate on file with 3LL.

4.2 Lapse in Coverage

Any lapse in required insurance coverage — even for a single day — will result in immediate suspension of the carrier from 3LL's approved carrier list. The carrier will not be permitted to haul loads for 3LL until: (a) proof of reinstated or new insurance is provided; (b) the certificate naming 3LL as additional insured is received; and (c) 3LL has verified the coverage through the insurer or carrier monitoring platform.

4.3 Mid-Year Policy Changes

If a carrier's insurance policy is cancelled, modified, or changed at any time during the policy year, the carrier must notify 3LL within twenty-four (24) hours and provide an updated Certificate of Insurance reflecting the new or modified coverage. Failure to notify 3LL of a mid-year policy change that results in 3LL dispatching a load without adequate carrier insurance coverage may result in the carrier being held liable for any resulting losses.

4.4 Renewal Tracking

3LL uses carrier monitoring software to track insurance expiration dates and will send automated renewal reminders to carriers approximately sixty (60) days before a certificate's expiration date. However, it is the carrier's responsibility to maintain current insurance and provide timely renewals — receipt of a reminder from 3LL is not a substitute for the carrier's own compliance obligations.

SECTION 5 — PROHIBITED EQUIPMENT AND CARGO TYPES

5.1 Equipment Restrictions

3LL will not dispatch loads on equipment that:

  • Is subject to an active out-of-service order;
  • Has failed a FMCSA roadside inspection and has not been reinspected and cleared;
  • Does not have a compliant ELD installed and operational (for loads requiring ELD);
  • Does not meet FMCSA inspection, maintenance, and safety standards under 49 CFR Parts 393 and 396;
  • Has been identified by 3LL's carrier monitoring service as associated with elevated accident or violation history without adequate remediation.

5.2 Cargo Restrictions — Standard Policy

3LL's standard Broker-Carrier Agreement and rate confirmations include restrictions on certain cargo types. Carriers must confirm their insurance coverage includes all relevant commodity types before accepting loads. Common cargo restrictions on standard cargo policies that carriers must disclose include:

5.3 Carrier Responsibility to Disclose Exclusions

It is the carrier's responsibility to review its cargo insurance policy for any commodity exclusions and to disclose all such exclusions to 3LL in writing before accepting any load. A carrier that accepts a load for a commodity excluded from its cargo policy and subsequently suffers a loss may be held personally liable for the full value of the loss.

SECTION 6 — CONSEQUENCES OF NON-COMPLIANCE

Carriers that fail to maintain required insurance or fail to provide current documentation will face the following consequences:

  • Immediate suspension from 3LL's approved carrier list pending resolution;
  • Withholding of pending settlements until compliance is confirmed;
  • Cancellation of any active load tendering arrangements;
  • Removal from 3LL's carrier database;
  • Potential liability for any cargo claims, bodily injury claims, or other damages arising from loads hauled without required coverage;
  • Reporting to FMCSA or carrier compliance monitoring services where appropriate.

SECTION 7 — CARRIER ACKNOWLEDGMENT

By completing and signing this Carrier Insurance Requirements Acknowledgment, the carrier confirms that:

  • The carrier has read and understands all insurance requirements set forth in this document;
  • The carrier currently maintains, or will maintain prior to hauling any load for 3LL, all required insurance coverages at the specified minimum limits;
  • The carrier's insurance policies name 3 Lakes Logistics LLC as an additional insured on the required coverage types;
  • The carrier will provide 3LL with immediate notice of any lapse, cancellation, or material change in coverage;
  • The carrier will provide updated Certificates of Insurance annually and upon any policy change;
  • The carrier has disclosed all cargo exclusions in its current cargo insurance policy.
