FAQ

Questions We Get
Before Day One.

Do my drivers need a CDL?

No. Light fleet drivers only need a standard driver's license, a clean MVR, and a clear background check. We handle all the verification — no FMCSA filing, no DOT medical card required.

Can I run both trucking and light fleet under one account?

Yes — the Trucking Add-On plan connects both operations to the same Eagle Eye dashboard. You see heavy trucks and car/SUV trips in one view, with one payout system for all drivers.

How does NEMT billing actually work?

We track the insurance authorization number and trip details per ride. At billing time, your completed trips are queued with the required documentation (pickup, dropoff, patient ID, auth code) ready for Medicaid/Medicare submission. Pro and Add-On plans include a 30-min billing setup call.

What vehicles qualify?

Sedans, SUVs, cargo vans, and luxury vehicles. For NEMT, wheelchair-accessible vans require documentation of the WAV setup. For executive, we support SUV, sedan, and luxury classes. Courier runs on any vehicle type.

Do you provide the drivers?

No — we're the dispatch and management platform. You bring your drivers (or hire them), we screen them and manage the operation. Think of us as your back office, dispatch center, and compliance team combined.

How fast can I go live?

Submit your drivers today, and the ones who pass MVR and background screening can be dispatching within 24 hours. Your Eagle Eye account is activated the same day you sign up. NEMT billing setup takes one additional 30-min call.

Is there a minimum number of drivers?

No minimum. You can start with 1 driver on the Starter plan and scale up. The Pro Fleet plan makes the most sense once you have 3+ active drivers running regular trips.

What happens if a driver's MVR comes back flagged?

Flagged drivers are held in a review queue. Our team contacts you within one business day to walk through the violation. Minor infractions may still qualify — serious violations (DUI, reckless driving) are automatic disqualifications for NEMT and executive runs.
