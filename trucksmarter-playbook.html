The Opportunity — What Is TruckSmarter?
🎯 The One-Sentence Summary
TruckSmarter is a free trucking super-app used by hundreds of thousands of owner-operators and small carriers — they need brokers to post loads AND they need carrier tech to monetize their user base. We have both.
What TruckSmarter Is📱
A VC-backed ($20M+ raised) free trucking app built for owner-operators. Features a free load board, fuel card, factoring, insurance, and business tools — all in one mobile app for drivers who run 1–5 trucks.
Their Business Model💰
They give the load board away FREE to build a massive carrier audience, then monetize that audience through fuel cards, factoring, insurance, and premium tools — exactly like how apps make money on services, not subscriptions.
💡 Why This Matters for Us
TruckSmarter already has the audience we need — owner-operators actively looking for loads. We have two things they need: (1) loads to put on their board from 3LL, and (2) carrier tech they can sell their users through IEBC. This is a two-channel deal, not just a lead generation play.
2
The Four Revenue Plays
What we get out of this
Play 1 — Post 3LL Loads (Free)🚚
Sign up as an approved freight broker on TruckSmarter and post 3LL loads directly to their driver network. Zero cost. Gets us carrier coverage on lanes without paying DAT/Truckstop fees.
💵 Saves $300–$500/mo in board fees. Faster coverage on hard lanes.
Play 2 — Carrier Recruitment🎯
Every driver who books a 3LL load through TruckSmarter enters our automated carrier onboarding sequence. Eagle Eye captures them, sends agreements, and adds them to our preferred carrier network.
💵 Low-cost carrier acquisition. Every new carrier = reduced coverage costs long-term.
Play 3 — IEBC Tech Partnership🤖
IEBC's carrier ops suite (dispatch automation, documents, settlement, onboarding, mobile app) gets offered to TruckSmarter's user base. TruckSmarter sells it, marks it up, keeps the margin — we get wholesale recurring revenue.
💵 Recurring monthly revenue per carrier user. 100 carriers = $5K–$15K/mo wholesale.
Play 4 — Eagle Eye Integration⚡
Connect Eagle Eye directly to TruckSmarter's API. 3LL loads auto-post to TruckSmarter when entered. Carrier responses come back into Eagle Eye's inbox. Bond Office AI agents handle coverage automatically.
💵 Eliminates manual board posting. AI runs coverage 24/7 without a dispatcher.

Step-by-Step Execution — Week 1
Do these in order
🔴 Cecilia — Start Here. Do These First.
The steps below are in priority order. Complete the broker signup first (it takes a few days to approve), then run the LinkedIn and email outreach simultaneously. Do not wait on the tech conversation until broker approval is confirmed.
1
Do Today
Sign Up as a Broker on TruckSmarter
Go to trucksmarter.com → look for "For Brokers," "Broker Portal," or "Post Loads" in the navigation or footer.

You will need: 3LL's MC number, DOT number, company name, and your contact email. Fill out the broker application completely. Use cece@3lakeslogistics.com as the account email so all carrier communications route to Eagle Eye.

Approval typically takes 1–3 business days. Once approved, you'll get login credentials to their broker posting portal.
2
Do Today
Find the Right Contact on LinkedIn
Go to LinkedIn.com → search "TruckSmarter" → click the company → click "People."

Search for titles: "Head of Partnerships," "VP Business Development," "Director of Partnerships," "Head of Growth," "BD Manager."

If you find a direct match, send a connection request using the LinkedIn message script in Section 4 of this guide. If no clear BD contact exists, look for the CEO or COO — TruckSmarter is a startup, so the founders may handle partnerships directly.

Write down the name and title before moving to Step 3.
3
Do Today
Find Their Direct Email Address
Try these in order:

1. Check their website footer/contact page for a partnerships or business email
2. Try partnerships@trucksmarter.com and bd@trucksmarter.com — common formats for startups
3. Use hunter.io (free plan) — type "trucksmarter.com" to find verified email addresses on their domain
4. Look at their LinkedIn "About" section — some companies list an email there

Once found, save it. You'll use it in Step 5.

This does two things: Gets us immediate carrier coverage AND gives us proof that we're already an active broker on TruckSmarter when we talk to their partnerships team. It makes us a real partner, not just a prospect.
