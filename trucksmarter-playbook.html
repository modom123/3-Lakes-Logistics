TruckSmarter — What They've Actually Built
Intelligence brief
📱 The Platform at a Glance
TruckSmarter is a VC-backed ($20M+ raised) free trucking super-app. Their load board aggregates 100,000+ daily loads from major brokers (C.H. Robinson, Spot, Armstrong, and 100+ others). Unlike DAT or Truckstop, the load board is completely free — they monetize downstream via freight factoring at 2.5–3% for same-day payouts and carrier services.
🤖 Their $49/Month AI Dispatch Tool
TruckSmarter's AI Dispatch assistant monitors lanes 24/7 based on carrier preferences (RPM, max deadhead, weight, trailer type). It actively:

✓ Calls brokers to verify if loads are still live
✓ Navigates phone trees and holds
✓ Sends professional bid emails on carrier's behalf
✓ Filters and surfaces the best matching freight

Price: $49/month per carrier.
❌ What Their AI Does NOT Do
TruckSmarter's AI stops the moment the broker responds. After that, everything is manual:

✗ Rate negotiation (multi-step back-and-forth)
✗ Contract and rate confirmation generation
✗ BOL and dispatch documentation
✗ Multi-dispatcher TMS backend
✗ Invoicing and carrier settlement
✗ Persistent memory across negotiations
✗ Enterprise multi-fleet operations

This is the gap IEBC fills.
🔗 Their Partner Ecosystem
Parade: TruckSmarter integrates with Parade (broker-side capacity management). Brokers post a load to Parade → it instantly pushes to TruckSmarter carriers for "Book Now" execution. This is a key integration channel for 3LL once we're licensed.

TCS Fuel Cards: Partnered to embed route-level fuel intelligence into their AI dispatch tool — carriers see true net profit (gross rate minus exact fuel cost per route) before booking. This is the type of embedded service partnership model TruckSmarter actively pursues.

Account Aggregation: Carriers can connect DAT, Truckstop, and broker portals directly into TruckSmarter — all loads in one unified search. This creates a clean API integration opportunity for IEBC.

The Three Partnership Models
Pick the right angle for each conversation
🏆 Model A — White-Label "TruckSmarter Pro" (Best Outcome)
TruckSmarter offers IEBC's Phase 2 AI suite as a premium tier above their $49/mo tool — branded as "TruckSmarter Pro" or "TruckSmarter Complete." They set the price ($149–$299/mo), collect it, pay IEBC a wholesale rate, keep the margin. Their users get a complete end-to-end automated freight operation. TruckSmarter gains a new recurring revenue stream without building anything. IEBC gets distribution to hundreds of thousands of carriers.
🔗 Model B — Preferred Tech Partner (Most Likely First Step)
IEBC is listed as a recommended/preferred tool partner on TruckSmarter's partner page — similar to how TCS Fuel Cards is featured. TruckSmarter promotes IEBC to their user base as the "back-office AI" that pairs with TruckSmarter's dispatch tool. Revenue flows to IEBC directly. TruckSmarter earns referral fees or affiliate revenue per signup. This is an easier agreement to reach and creates proof before pursuing Model A.
⚡ Model C — API Integration Partnership (Technical Angle)
IEBC builds a direct integration between Eagle Eye and TruckSmarter's load feed API. TruckSmarter's load data flows into Eagle Eye's dispatch agents in real time. IEBC can use this to demonstrate technical depth AND propose that TruckSmarter resell the integration as a premium feature to enterprise carrier accounts and small fleet operators.

Technical Integration — How It Would Work
For reference on the call — Mark leads this conversation
📋 Cecilia: You Do Not Need to Explain This on the Call
This section is background for you to understand what's possible. If TruckSmarter asks technical questions, say "Our CEO Mark Odom is our technical lead — I'll bring him into the next conversation to walk through the integration specifics." Do not attempt to explain API architecture yourself.
Integration Architecture — Eagle Eye ↔ TruckSmarter
Load Feed
TruckSmarter's web portal runs on structured GraphQL/REST APIs. Their load data can be pulled programmatically into Eagle Eye's dispatch agents, giving IEBC access to 100K+ daily loads without paying DAT or Truckstop fees.
RPA Option
If API access isn't part of the partnership, an n8n RPA automation flow can aggregate TruckSmarter search results and feed them into Eagle Eye — a zero-cost integration approach that doesn't require TruckSmarter's cooperation.
Handoff Point
TruckSmarter AI sends the bid email. Broker responds. Eagle Eye's Claude-powered agents take the thread — they handle negotiation, generate rate confirmation, and pass to dispatch queue automatically.
TMS Backend
Eagle Eye runs the multi-dispatcher TMS layer TruckSmarter doesn't have — load board, dispatch coordination, carrier comms, BOL tracking, and settlement, all in one platform.
Memory Layer
Supabase + pgvector gives IEBC's agents persistent memory — they remember carrier preferences, negotiation history, and relationship context across every interaction. TruckSmarter has no equivalent.
