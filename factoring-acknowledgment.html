SECTION 1 — NOTICE OF ASSIGNMENT

1.1 Assignment of Receivables

Carrier hereby notifies 3 Lakes Logistics LLC that Carrier has entered into a factoring agreement with the Factor named above, pursuant to which Carrier has assigned, sold, and transferred to Factor all of Carrier's right, title, and interest in and to freight receivables arising from transportation services rendered by Carrier to or through 3 Lakes Logistics LLC, including but not limited to amounts due under rate confirmations, load tenders, and dispatch agreements.

1.2 Direction to Pay Factor

Carrier hereby irrevocably directs 3 Lakes Logistics LLC to remit all payments for freight services rendered by Carrier — including base rates, fuel surcharges, detention pay, accessorial charges, and any other compensation — directly to the Factor at the payment address specified in Section 2 of this Agreement. This direction to pay shall remain in effect until 3LL receives written notice of termination from both the Carrier and the Factor.

1.3 Acknowledgment by 3 Lakes Logistics LLC

By processing loads under this Agreement, 3 Lakes Logistics LLC acknowledges receipt of this Notice of Assignment and agrees to direct all payments for covered freight services to the Factor. 3LL's acknowledgment does not constitute a waiver of any defense or offset 3LL may have against Carrier, nor does it create any obligation greater than 3LL's original obligation to Carrier.

SECTION 3 — CARRIER REPRESENTATIONS

Carrier represents and warrants to 3 Lakes Logistics LLC that:

  • Carrier has entered into a valid factoring agreement with the Factor and has the authority to assign its receivables;
  • The Factor has been duly notified and has accepted the assignment of receivables arising from loads tendered by 3LL;
  • Carrier will not attempt to collect payment directly from 3LL for any factored load without the written consent of the Factor;
  • Carrier will promptly notify 3LL in writing if the factoring relationship with the Factor is terminated;
  • Carrier will provide 3LL with updated payment instructions within 48 hours of any change in factoring company or payment routing.

SECTION 4 — 3LL PROTECTIONS

4.1 No Double Payment

Once 3LL has remitted payment to the Factor in accordance with this Agreement, 3LL shall have no further payment obligation to Carrier for the applicable load, regardless of any dispute between Carrier and Factor. Carrier shall indemnify and hold 3LL harmless from any claims arising from double-payment demands.

4.2 Offsets and Deductions

3LL reserves the right to offset from any payment to the Factor any amounts owed by Carrier to 3LL, including cargo claims, fuel advance repayments, chargebacks, and any other authorized deductions, to the same extent 3LL would have the right to offset such amounts against direct payments to Carrier.

4.3 Disputes

Any dispute regarding the amount owed for a specific load shall be resolved between 3LL and Carrier in accordance with the applicable Carrier Dispatch Agreement. The Factor's rights are limited to the net amount actually owed by 3LL to Carrier for the applicable load after all authorized offsets and deductions.

4.4 Change of Factor

If Carrier changes factoring companies, Carrier must notify 3LL in writing at least 5 business days before the change takes effect and provide a new executed Factoring Acknowledgment before any load settlements will be redirected to the new Factor.

SECTION 5 — TERMINATION

This Notice of Assignment and the payment direction contained herein shall remain in effect until: (a) 3LL receives written notice signed by both Carrier and Factor that the factoring agreement has been terminated; and (b) 3LL has had a reasonable opportunity (not less than 5 business days) to update its payment records. Until both conditions are met, 3LL will continue to direct payments to the Factor.

SECTION 6 — GOVERNING LAW

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of California and applicable provisions of the Uniform Commercial Code (UCC Article 9) governing assignment of receivables. Disputes shall be resolved in Kern County, California.
