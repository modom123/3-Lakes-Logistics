**Plan period:** October 2026 – September 2029 (six 6-month segments)

## 1. What we sell (single price sheet — `backend/app/pricing.py`)

| Customer | Plan | Price | 3LL earns per truck/driver per month* |
|---|---|---|---|
| CDL owner-operator | **Pay-As-You-Go** | 8% of each load, no monthly | ~$1,600 (8 loads × $2,500 × 8%) |
| CDL owner-operator | **Founders** (first 1,000 trucks) | $300/mo per truck, 0% per load, locked for life | $300 |
| CDL owner-operator | **Pro** | $500/mo per truck, 0% per load | $500 |
| Van / car / NEMT / courier | **Light Fleet** | 15% per completed trip | ~$400 |
| IEBC / TruckSmarter channel | **FALCON** | 15% per load | ~$3,000 |

\*Rough averages for a full-time unit, used for planning only.

> **Pricing warning — decide this before you scale:** a full-time truck pays us about **5× more on
> Pay-As-You-Go ($1,600) than on Founders ($300)**. Any carrier running steady will do that math and
> switch to Founders. That's fine for signing people up (it's an easy yes), but Founders at $300 only
> makes money at volume with the AI doing the work. Selling point for Friday: **lead with
> Pay-As-You-Go** (no risk to the carrier, no contract). Offer Founders when a carrier asks for a lower
> price or has been with us 60+ days.

## 3. How leads are covered

| Source | What it does | Status |
|---|---|---|
| **FMCSA census pull** (Naomi, 07:15 and 18:00 UTC daily) | Authorized carriers with 1–10 trucks in the top 5 states, **newest authority first** | Live; this change retargets it from big fleets to our ideal customer |
| **Today's calls bar** | Counts calls logged today against the 25 target and shows how many days of leads are waiting | New |
| **Lead top-up** (weekdays 11:30 + 17:30 UTC) | Refills the pool to 3 days of calls from 20 states | New |
| **Eagle Eye → Call List** | Ranks callable leads (phone present, not do-not-contact, follow-up due) by score, then by newest authority | New |
| **Call logging** | 9 outcomes. Each one sets the status and the next follow-up date. "Do not call" is permanent | New |
| **CSV export** | Timestamped spreadsheet for calling from your cell phone | New |
| **Website intake** | Founders / Pro / Pay-As-You-Go sign-ups go straight to onboarding and checkout | Live |
| **Email campaigns** | Cold email with unsubscribe (CAN-SPAM) | Live |
| **AI calls / SMS** (Vance, SMS campaigner) | Automated outreach | Turn **off** for cold leads (see TCPA above) |
| **Referrals** | "Month 1 free" for a referred carrier | Copy fixed; start asking every signed carrier |

**Lead math at 25 calls/day:** 25 × ~21 weekdays ≈ 525 calls/month. At a 1–2% close rate that's
**5–10 new carriers/month per caller**. That's enough for Segment 1 (25 trucks by March). Add a second caller or
raise `DAILY_CALL_TARGET` to grow faster.

**How the system keeps 25 calls a day supplied:**
- Every weekday at 11:30 UTC (7:30am Eastern) and 17:30 UTC, `fire_lead_supply` counts callable leads.
  If fewer than **75** are waiting (3 days × 25), it pulls more FMCSA carriers from 20 high-volume states,
  4 states at a time, until the buffer is full.
- The Call List page shows **Today's calls: X of 25** and **N leads waiting (D days)**. It turns red when
  there are fewer than 25 waiting. **Pull new FMCSA leads** runs the same top-up on demand.
- A carrier you called won't come back up until its follow-up date. Do-not-call is permanent. This
  ensures that each day's 25 are mostly fresh numbers plus the callbacks that are due.
- The target and buffer are settings: `DAILY_CALL_TARGET=25`, `LEAD_BUFFER_DAYS=3`.

## 4. Three-year plan — six 6-month segments

Revenue figures are **monthly revenue at the end of the segment**, using the blended per-unit
numbers above. They are targets to steer by, not forecasts. Each segment has a **gate**: don't start the
next segment's spending until you've hit it.

### Segment 1 — Oct 2026 → Mar 2027 · "Prove it"
- **Goal:** real carriers, real loads, real payments.
- **Units:** 25 trucks + 10 light fleet drivers.
- **Mix:** mostly Pay-As-You-Go.
- **Exit monthly revenue:** ~$15K–$25K.
- **Work:** founders hand-dial daily, book every load personally in DAT, run weekly settlements, and fix whatever breaks in the software.
- **Spend:** DAT, Render/Supabase, Stripe fees. No hires.
- **Gate:** 20+ trucks active 60+ days, less than 10% monthly churn, and payments collected on time.

### Segment 2 — Apr 2027 → Sep 2027 · "Make it repeatable"
- **Goal:** someone other than the founders can run a day.
- **Units:** 60 trucks + 25 light fleet drivers.
- **Exit monthly revenue:** ~$35K–$50K.
- **Work:**
  - Hire dispatcher #1 (commission plus base).
  - Write down every dispatch step.
  - Connect a load board API (DAT or Truckstop) to Load Hunter.
  - Launch the referral program.
  - Collect SMS/call consent on the website so AI outreach can run legally.
- **Gate:** one dispatcher runs 25+ trucks with no founder involvement, and customer acquisition cost is under 2 months of revenue.

### Segment 3 — Oct 2027 → Mar 2028 · "Automate"
- **Goal:** software does the routine work; people handle exceptions.
- **Units:** 120 trucks + 50 light fleet drivers.
- **Exit monthly revenue:** ~$70K–$90K.
- **Work:**
  - Turn on the AI features for real traffic: AI check calls, auto rate cons, and BOL/POD processing.
  - Run AI outbound only to opted-in leads.
  - Add a factoring partner revenue share (see `factoring-acknowledgment.html`).
  - Hire a second dispatcher and a part-time compliance/accounting person.
- **Gate:** at least 40 trucks per dispatcher, and gross margin over 60%.

### Segment 4 — Apr 2028 → Sep 2028 · "Scale acquisition"
- **Goal:** predictable growth engine.
- **Units:** 250 trucks + 100 light fleet drivers.
- **Exit monthly revenue:** ~$130K–$160K.
- **Work:** 2–3 inside sales reps, paid ads to new-authority carriers, trade shows (MATS March 2028), and NEMT and medical courier contracts for light fleet.
- **Gate:** at least 100 new trucks per quarter, and 12-month retention over 70%.

### Segment 5 — Oct 2028 → Mar 2029 · "Expand the business lines"
- **Goal:** more revenue per customer.
- **Units:** 450 trucks + 200 light fleet drivers.
- **Exit monthly revenue:** ~$220K–$280K.
- **Work:**
  - Open North American Trading as a licensed brokerage (own MC and BMC-84 bond) for shipper freight run on our own carriers.
  - Fuel card and insurance referral revenue.
  - IEBC consulting and white-label deals (TruckSmarter model in `trucksmarter-playbook.html`).
- **Gate:** brokerage profitable on its own, and less than 20% of revenue from any single broker or shipper.

### Segment 6 — Apr 2029 → Sep 2029 · "Founders cap and enterprise"
- **Goal:** fill the 1,000 Founders spots and move new sign-ups to Pro.
- **Units:** 700–1,000 trucks + 300 light fleet drivers.
- **Exit monthly revenue:** ~$350K–$450K (~$4–5M/year run rate).
- **Work:** close Founders when the categories sell out, offer enterprise contracts to 10–50-truck fleets, and decide whether to raise money or keep growing on revenue.
