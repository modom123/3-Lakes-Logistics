SECTION 1 — ENGAGEMENT AND INDEPENDENT CONTRACTOR RELATIONSHIP

1.1 Engagement

The Company hereby engages the Contractor, and the Contractor hereby accepts such engagement, to perform the services described in Section 2 of this Agreement (the "Services"), subject to the terms and conditions set forth herein.

1.2 Independent Contractor Status

The Contractor is an independent contractor and not an employee, agent, partner, or joint venturer of the Company. The Parties expressly acknowledge and agree that:

  • The Contractor shall have the right to control and direct the means and methods by which the Services are performed, subject to the requirement that the Services meet the Company's standards and deliverable requirements;
  • The Contractor shall not be subject to the supervision or control of the Company with respect to the manner in which the Services are performed, except that the Company may require the Services to be performed in a particular manner to comply with applicable law, regulation, or client requirements;
  • The Contractor is free to perform services for other clients and businesses so long as such activities do not create a conflict of interest or violate any provision of this Agreement;
  • The Company shall not provide the Contractor with employee benefits of any kind, including but not limited to health insurance, retirement benefits, vacation pay, sick pay, disability insurance, or workers' compensation coverage;
  • The Company shall not withhold income taxes, Social Security taxes, Medicare taxes, or any other taxes from payments made to the Contractor. The Contractor is solely responsible for payment of all applicable taxes on compensation received under this Agreement.

1.3 ABC Test Acknowledgment

The Parties acknowledge the California Supreme Court's decision in Dynamex Operations West, Inc. v. Superior Court and the provisions of California Assembly Bill 5 (AB 5) as codified in California Labor Code Section 2775 et seq. The Contractor represents and warrants that:

  • (A) The Contractor is free from the control and direction of the Company in connection with the performance of the Services, both under this Agreement and in fact;
  • (B) The Contractor performs work that is outside the usual course of the Company's business, or the Contractor customarily engages in an independently established trade, occupation, or business of the same nature as the work performed; and
  • (C) The Contractor is customarily engaged in an independently established trade, occupation, or business.

1.4 No Authority to Bind

The Contractor shall have no authority to enter into contracts or agreements on behalf of the Company, to incur any obligation or liability on behalf of the Company, or to represent or hold himself/herself/itself out as an agent or employee of the Company, unless expressly authorized in writing by an authorized officer of the Company.

3.2 Payment Terms — Net-15

The Company shall pay all undisputed invoices within fifteen (15) calendar days of receipt of a properly submitted invoice. The Contractor shall submit invoices no more frequently than twice per calendar month unless otherwise agreed. Invoices must include: Contractor's name and contact information, description of services performed, dates of service, applicable load numbers or project identifiers, and the total amount due.

3.3 Disputed Invoices

If the Company disputes any portion of an invoice, the Company shall notify the Contractor in writing within five (5) business days of receipt of the invoice, identifying the disputed amount and the basis for the dispute. The Parties shall work in good faith to resolve any such dispute within ten (10) business days of such notification. Undisputed portions of invoices shall be paid within the Net-15 payment terms.

3.4 Expense Reimbursement

The Company shall reimburse the Contractor for reasonable and pre-approved out-of-pocket expenses incurred in connection with the performance of the Services, provided that: (a) such expenses were pre-approved in writing by an authorized representative of the Company; (b) the Contractor submits receipts or other documentation of such expenses; and (c) such expenses are submitted for reimbursement within thirty (30) days of being incurred.

3.5 Tax Obligations

The Contractor acknowledges sole responsibility for payment of all federal, state, and local income taxes, self-employment taxes, and any other taxes or assessments on compensation received under this Agreement. The Company will issue a Form 1099-NEC to the Contractor for each calendar year in which the Contractor receives compensation of $600 or more from the Company, as required by applicable law. The Contractor shall provide the Company with a completed IRS Form W-9 prior to the commencement of Services.

3.6 No Benefits

The Contractor acknowledges and agrees that the Contractor is not entitled to participate in any employee benefit plan, program, or arrangement of the Company, including but not limited to health, dental, vision, or life insurance; retirement or 401(k) plans; paid vacation, sick leave, or holiday pay; short-term or long-term disability; or workers' compensation insurance.

SECTION 4 — INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

4.1 Work Made for Hire

To the extent permitted by applicable law, all work product, inventions, discoveries, developments, improvements, writings, software, data, documentation, reports, analyses, and other materials created, conceived, or developed by the Contractor in connection with the performance of Services under this Agreement (collectively, "Work Product") shall be considered "work made for hire" as defined under the United States Copyright Act (17 U.S.C. § 101) and shall be the sole and exclusive property of the Company from the moment of creation.

4.2 Assignment

To the extent any Work Product does not qualify as "work made for hire" under applicable law, the Contractor hereby irrevocably assigns to the Company all right, title, and interest in and to such Work Product, including all intellectual property rights therein, throughout the world, in perpetuity. The Contractor agrees to execute all documents and take all actions reasonably requested by the Company to evidence, perfect, or enforce such assignment.

4.3 Pre-Existing Intellectual Property

The Contractor shall retain ownership of any tools, methodologies, processes, inventions, or other intellectual property developed by the Contractor prior to the Effective Date of this Agreement ("Pre-Existing IP"). If any Work Product incorporates Pre-Existing IP, the Contractor hereby grants the Company a non-exclusive, royalty-free, perpetual, irrevocable license to use such Pre-Existing IP as incorporated in the Work Product.

4.4 No Use of Company IP

The Contractor shall not use any of the Company's trademarks, trade names, logos, or other intellectual property for any purpose other than performing the Services under this Agreement, without the prior written consent of the Company.

SECTION 5 — CONFIDENTIALITY

5.1 Definition of Confidential Information

For purposes of this Agreement, "Confidential Information" means any and all information, data, or material of the Company or its clients that is proprietary, confidential, or not generally known to the public, including but not limited to: customer and carrier lists; pricing, rate, and margin information; business strategies and plans; financial information; employee and contractor information; proprietary software, systems, and processes; load data and shipment records; and any other information marked as confidential or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential given the nature of the information and the circumstances of disclosure.

5.2 Obligations

The Contractor agrees to: (a) hold all Confidential Information in strict confidence; (b) not disclose any Confidential Information to any third party without the prior written consent of the Company; (c) use Confidential Information solely for the purpose of performing the Services under this Agreement; (d) take all reasonable precautions to protect the confidentiality of the Confidential Information; and (e) immediately notify the Company upon becoming aware of any unauthorized disclosure or use of Confidential Information.

5.3 Exceptions

The confidentiality obligations set forth in this Section shall not apply to information that: (a) is or becomes publicly available through no fault of the Contractor; (b) was already known to the Contractor prior to disclosure by the Company, as evidenced by written records; (c) is independently developed by the Contractor without use of or reference to the Confidential Information; or (d) is required to be disclosed by applicable law, regulation, or court order, provided that the Contractor gives the Company prompt written notice of such requirement and cooperates with the Company's efforts to seek a protective order.

5.4 Return of Materials

Upon termination of this Agreement for any reason, or upon the Company's request at any time, the Contractor shall promptly return to the Company all Confidential Information and all materials containing Confidential Information, or certify in writing that all such materials have been destroyed.

5.5 Survival

The confidentiality obligations set forth in this Section 5 shall survive the termination or expiration of this Agreement for a period of three (3) years.

SECTION 6 — NON-SOLICITATION

6.1 Non-Solicitation of Clients

During the term of this Agreement and for a period of twelve (12) months following the termination or expiration of this Agreement for any reason (the "Restricted Period"), the Contractor shall not, directly or indirectly:

  • Solicit, induce, or encourage any customer, shipper, or client of the Company (with whom the Contractor had contact, or about whom the Contractor received Confidential Information, during the term of this Agreement) to reduce, terminate, or divert any of its business with the Company;
  • Provide to any such customer, shipper, or client services that are the same as or substantially similar to the Services performed by the Contractor for the Company under this Agreement.

6.2 Non-Solicitation of Employees and Contractors

During the Restricted Period, the Contractor shall not, directly or indirectly, solicit, recruit, hire, or encourage any employee, officer, or independent contractor of the Company to terminate their relationship with the Company or to work for the Contractor or any other person or entity.

6.3 Non-Solicitation of Carriers

During the Restricted Period, the Contractor shall not, directly or indirectly, solicit, induce, or encourage any carrier that was referred to the Contractor by the Company or with whom the Contractor made contact through the Company's systems, platforms, or business relationships to divert freight from the Company or to establish a direct relationship that bypasses the Company.

6.4 Reasonableness

The Contractor acknowledges that the restrictions contained in this Section 6 are reasonable and necessary to protect the Company's legitimate business interests, including its Confidential Information, customer relationships, and goodwill. If any court of competent jurisdiction determines that any provision of this Section 6 is unenforceable, the Parties authorize such court to modify such provision to the minimum extent necessary to make it enforceable.

SECTION 8 — INDEMNIFICATION

8.1 Indemnification by Contractor

The Contractor shall indemnify, defend, and hold harmless the Company and its members, managers, officers, employees, agents, and successors (collectively, "Company Indemnitees") from and against any and all claims, damages, losses, liabilities, costs, and expenses (including reasonable attorneys' fees) arising out of or related to: (a) the Contractor's performance of or failure to perform the Services; (b) the Contractor's breach of any representation, warranty, covenant, or obligation under this Agreement; (c) the Contractor's negligence or willful misconduct; or (d) any claim by a third party that the Contractor is an employee of the Company.

8.2 Indemnification by Company

The Company shall indemnify, defend, and hold harmless the Contractor from and against any and all claims, damages, losses, liabilities, costs, and expenses (including reasonable attorneys' fees) arising out of or related to: (a) the Company's breach of any representation, warranty, covenant, or obligation under this Agreement; or (b) the Company's negligence or willful misconduct.

SECTION 9 — LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

EXCEPT FOR LIABILITY ARISING FROM INDEMNIFICATION OBLIGATIONS, BREACH OF CONFIDENTIALITY, OR INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY OBLIGATIONS, IN NO EVENT SHALL EITHER PARTY BE LIABLE TO THE OTHER FOR ANY INDIRECT, INCIDENTAL, SPECIAL, CONSEQUENTIAL, OR PUNITIVE DAMAGES ARISING OUT OF OR RELATED TO THIS AGREEMENT, EVEN IF SUCH PARTY HAS BEEN ADVISED OF THE POSSIBILITY OF SUCH DAMAGES. THE TOTAL LIABILITY OF EITHER PARTY FOR ANY CLAIM ARISING UNDER OR RELATED TO THIS AGREEMENT SHALL NOT EXCEED THE TOTAL AMOUNT PAID OR PAYABLE BY THE COMPANY TO THE CONTRACTOR IN THE THREE (3) MONTHS PRECEDING THE CLAIM.

SECTION 10 — TERM AND TERMINATION

10.1 Term

This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and shall continue until terminated by either Party in accordance with the provisions of this Section 10, unless earlier terminated for cause.

10.2 Termination Without Cause

Either Party may terminate this Agreement without cause upon thirty (30) days prior written notice to the other Party. During such notice period, the Contractor shall continue to perform the Services as directed by the Company, and the Company shall continue to pay the Contractor for Services actually performed.

10.3 Termination for Cause

Either Party may terminate this Agreement immediately upon written notice to the other Party if:

  • The other Party materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure such breach within ten (10) days after receipt of written notice describing the breach in reasonable detail;
  • The other Party becomes insolvent, makes a general assignment for the benefit of creditors, or becomes the subject of a voluntary or involuntary bankruptcy proceeding;
  • The Contractor engages in fraud, dishonesty, gross negligence, or willful misconduct in connection with the performance of the Services;
  • The Contractor violates any applicable law or regulation in connection with the performance of the Services.

10.4 Effect of Termination

Upon the termination or expiration of this Agreement: (a) the Contractor shall immediately cease performing the Services; (b) the Contractor shall deliver to the Company all Work Product, Confidential Information, and Company property in the Contractor's possession or control; (c) the Company shall pay the Contractor for all Services satisfactorily performed prior to the effective date of termination; and (d) all provisions of this Agreement that by their nature should survive termination shall survive, including Sections 4, 5, 6, 8, 9, and 11.

SECTION 11 — GENERAL PROVISIONS

11.1 Governing Law and Venue

This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of California, without regard to its conflict of laws provisions. Any dispute, claim, or controversy arising out of or relating to this Agreement shall be resolved exclusively in the state or federal courts located in California, and each Party hereby consents to the personal jurisdiction and venue of such courts.

11.2 Dispute Resolution

Prior to initiating any legal proceedings, the Parties agree to attempt to resolve any dispute through good-faith negotiation for a period of thirty (30) days. If the dispute cannot be resolved through negotiation, the Parties may pursue mediation before resorting to litigation.

11.3 Entire Agreement

This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties with respect to the subject matter hereof and supersedes all prior and contemporaneous agreements, representations, and understandings, whether written or oral, relating to such subject matter. No amendment to or modification of this Agreement shall be effective unless made in writing and signed by both Parties.

11.4 Severability

If any provision of this Agreement is held to be invalid, illegal, or unenforceable, such provision shall be modified to the minimum extent necessary to make it valid, legal, and enforceable, and the remaining provisions of this Agreement shall continue in full force and effect.

11.5 Waiver

No failure or delay by either Party in exercising any right under this Agreement shall operate as a waiver of such right. No waiver of any breach or default shall be deemed a waiver of any subsequent breach or default of the same or any other provision.

11.6 Notices

All notices required or permitted under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be delivered by: (a) personal delivery; (b) certified mail, return receipt requested, postage prepaid; (c) overnight courier; or (d) email with confirmation of receipt. Notices to the Company shall be sent to: info@3lakeslogistics.com. Notices to the Contractor shall be sent to the address or email address set forth in the preamble of this Agreement.

11.7 Assignment

The Contractor may not assign this Agreement or any rights or obligations hereunder without the prior written consent of the Company. The Company may assign this Agreement in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of all or substantially all of its assets, without the Contractor's consent.

11.8 Counterparts and Electronic Signatures

This Agreement may be executed in one or more counterparts, each of which shall be deemed an original. Electronic signatures shall be deemed valid and binding to the same extent as original signatures.

11.9 No Third-Party Beneficiaries

This Agreement is for the sole benefit of the Parties and their permitted successors and assigns. Nothing in this Agreement shall create or be deemed to create any rights in any third party.
