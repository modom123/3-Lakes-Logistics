Broker–Carrier Agreement

Agreement Version: 1.0  |  Effective Date: May 25, 2026

This is a legally binding agreement. Read every section carefully before signing. By executing this Agreement (electronically or in writing), both parties agree to all terms below. Questions? Email info@3lakeslogistics.com.

This Broker–Carrier Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into between 3 Lakes Logistics LLC, a California limited liability company acting in its capacity as a licensed or registered freight intermediary ("Broker"), and the motor carrier identified in the signature block below ("Carrier"). Together referred to as the "Parties."

1. Purpose & Scope

This Agreement governs the terms and conditions under which Broker will tender freight shipments to Carrier for transportation, and under which Carrier will accept and perform those transportation services. Broker is not a shipper or consignee and does not take possession of cargo. Carrier provides the actual transportation services as an independent contractor.

2. Carrier Qualifications

Carrier represents and warrants that, at all times during the term of this Agreement, it:

  • Holds active motor carrier authority issued by the Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration (FMCSA) with an active USDOT number and, where applicable, a valid MC number
  • Is not subject to a federal "Do Not Load" order, safety fitness rating of "Unsatisfactory," or conditional rating that prohibits transportation of the tendered freight
  • Maintains all required operating licenses, permits, and registrations for every state in which it operates
  • Employs only drivers holding valid Commercial Driver's Licenses (CDLs) appropriate to the vehicle class and cargo type
  • Complies with all FMCSA Hours of Service (HOS) regulations and is equipped with a certified Electronic Logging Device (ELD) as required by 49 CFR Part 395
  • Complies with all federal, state, and local laws and regulations governing the operation of commercial motor vehicles
  • Has not double-brokered, re-brokered, or assigned any shipment tendered under this Agreement without Broker's prior written consent

Carrier authorizes Broker to verify its compliance status through FMCSA's SAFER system and third-party monitoring services at any time. Carrier must notify Broker within 24 hours of any lapse in operating authority, insurance, or driver qualification.

3. Insurance Requirements

Carrier must maintain the following insurance coverage with carriers rated A- VII or better by AM Best, and must provide Broker with current Certificates of Insurance (COI) naming 3 Lakes Logistics LLC as a certificate holder:

Coverage Type Minimum Limit Requirement
Commercial Auto Liability $1,000,000 per occurrence Required — all loads
Motor Truck Cargo (MTC) $100,000 per occurrence Required — all loads
General Liability $1,000,000 per occurrence Required
Workers' Compensation Statutory limits As required by state law
Hazmat / Pollution Liability As required by FMCSA Required if hauling hazmat

Carrier must provide 30 days' advance written notice to Broker prior to cancellation or material reduction of any required coverage. Broker may immediately suspend load tenders if coverage lapses. Carrier's insurance is primary and non-contributory with respect to any claim arising from Carrier's operations. Broker maintains its own contingent cargo liability insurance.

4. Load Tenders & Rate Confirmations

4.1 Tender Process

Broker will tender loads to Carrier verbally, electronically, or via the 3 Lakes platform. Each load is governed by a Rate Confirmation ("RC"), which sets forth the specific load details, rate, and accessorials. A Rate Confirmation does not become binding until Carrier acknowledges acceptance in writing (email, platform, or fax) or by committing a driver/equipment to the load.

4.2 Rate Confirmation Controls

In the event of a conflict between any Rate Confirmation and this Agreement, the Rate Confirmation controls for load-specific terms (rate, dates, special instructions). This Agreement controls for all other matters (insurance, liability, payment terms, dispute resolution).

4.3 Right to Reject

Carrier may decline any tendered load before accepting the Rate Confirmation. Once a Rate Confirmation is accepted, Carrier is bound to perform the transportation services as described.

4.4 No Double-Brokering

Carrier shall not re-broker, sub-contract, or otherwise assign any load tendered under this Agreement to another motor carrier without Broker's prior written consent. Violation of this provision is grounds for immediate termination and Carrier shall be liable for all resulting damages, including cargo loss, claims, and Broker's costs.

5. Transportation Services

5.1 Performance Standards

Carrier agrees to:

  • Pick up and deliver cargo within the time windows specified on the Rate Confirmation
  • Provide a roadworthy, clean, and compliant vehicle appropriate for the tendered freight
  • Ensure the driver assigned to each load has reviewed and complied with all shipper and consignee requirements noted on the Rate Confirmation
  • Obtain a legible Proof of Delivery (POD) with shipper/consignee signature and provide it to Broker within 48 hours of delivery
  • Notify Broker immediately of delays, accidents, cargo events, or any circumstance that may affect timely delivery

5.2 Lumper & Accessorial Services

Any accessorials (detention, layover, TONU, lumper reimbursement) must be pre-authorized by Broker in writing before Carrier incurs the expense. Unauthorized accessorials will not be reimbursed. Detention time begins accruing after 2 free hours at shipper or receiver and is paid at the rate specified on the Rate Confirmation.

5.3 Exclusive Use

Unless the Rate Confirmation explicitly states otherwise, Carrier shall transport the tendered freight exclusively in a single vehicle and shall not co-load with other freight without Broker's written consent.

6. Freight Charges & Payment

6.1 All-In Rate

The rate specified on the Rate Confirmation is the complete, all-in compensation for the load, including fuel surcharge, unless line items are separately identified. No additional charges will be paid unless pre-authorized by Broker in writing.

6.2 Payment Terms

Broker will pay Carrier the agreed rate within 30 days of receipt of all required documentation: (a) signed Rate Confirmation, (b) legible POD, and (c) invoice. Quick Pay options (within 2–7 business days) may be available at a fee of 2–3%, subject to Broker's approval. Payments are made via ACH or check.

6.3 Invoice Requirements

Carrier's invoice must reference the Broker's load number and include all supporting documents. Invoices submitted more than 180 days after delivery date will not be honored unless otherwise agreed in writing.

6.4 Offset & Deductions

Broker may offset against any amounts owed to Carrier: (a) cargo claims; (b) overpayments from prior loads; (c) chargebacks authorized under this Agreement; or (d) amounts owed by Carrier under any other written agreement with Broker.

6.5 Disputed Charges

Carrier must dispute any payment in writing within 180 days of the payment date. Disputes submitted after this window are waived. Broker will respond to disputes within 30 days.

7. Cargo Liability & Claims

7.1 Carrier Liability

Carrier assumes full liability for loss, damage, or delay to cargo from the time it takes possession at the origin until delivery and receipt at the destination, subject to the defenses available under the Carmack Amendment (49 U.S.C. § 14706) and applicable regulations.

7.2 Liability Limit

Unless otherwise agreed in writing on the Rate Confirmation, Carrier's liability per shipment is limited to the actual value of the cargo, not to exceed the Carrier's cargo insurance policy limit. Carrier may not limit liability below the cargo value without Broker's express written consent on the Rate Confirmation.

7.3 Claim Reporting

Carrier must report any cargo exception (shortage, damage, refusal) to Broker within 24 hours of delivery. Written notice of a cargo claim must be submitted to Broker within 9 months of delivery. Legal action on a claim must be filed within 2 years of the date a written claim is denied.

7.4 Salvage

Carrier shall not dispose of, destroy, or abandon damaged cargo without Broker's written authorization. Carrier must take reasonable steps to preserve and protect damaged cargo pending claim resolution.

8. Shipper Instructions & Special Requirements

Carrier must comply with all shipper and consignee requirements noted on the Rate Confirmation, including temperature requirements for reefer loads, securement standards for specialized freight, and facility-specific check-in procedures. Carrier is responsible for ensuring its driver has all required certifications (e.g., TWIC, hazmat endorsement) before arriving at a facility where such credentials are required.

9. Confidentiality

Carrier agrees to keep confidential all information received from Broker in connection with this Agreement, including shipper and consignee identities, freight rates, load details, and customer relationships ("Confidential Information"). Carrier shall not use Confidential Information to solicit or conduct business directly with Broker's customers for a period of 12 months following termination of this Agreement, without Broker's prior written consent.

10. Independent Contractor Status

Carrier is an independent contractor. Nothing in this Agreement creates an employment, agency, joint venture, or partnership relationship. Carrier is solely responsible for all taxes, wages, benefits, and withholdings for its employees and subcontractors. Carrier shall not represent itself as an employee or agent of Broker.

11. Indemnification

Carrier agrees to indemnify, defend, and hold harmless Broker and its officers, directors, employees, and agents from and against all claims, damages, losses, costs, and expenses (including reasonable attorneys' fees) arising out of or relating to: (a) Carrier's transportation services; (b) any accident, injury, death, or property damage involving Carrier's vehicles or personnel; (c) any cargo claim or loss; (d) Carrier's violation of any law or regulation; (e) Carrier's breach of this Agreement; or (f) any act or omission of Carrier's employees, drivers, or subcontractors.

Broker agrees to indemnify Carrier for claims arising solely from Broker's own negligence or willful misconduct.

12. Limitation of Liability

BROKER'S TOTAL LIABILITY TO CARRIER FOR ANY CLAIM ARISING OUT OF THIS AGREEMENT SHALL NOT EXCEED THE FREIGHT CHARGES PAID FOR THE SPECIFIC LOAD GIVING RISE TO THE CLAIM. BROKER SHALL NOT BE LIABLE FOR INDIRECT, INCIDENTAL, SPECIAL, OR CONSEQUENTIAL DAMAGES, INCLUDING LOST PROFITS.

13. Term & Termination

13.1 Term

This Agreement is effective upon execution and continues until terminated by either party.

13.2 Termination for Convenience

Either party may terminate this Agreement with 30 days' written notice. Termination does not affect loads accepted prior to the notice date.

13.3 Immediate Termination

Broker may terminate this Agreement immediately, without notice, if Carrier: (a) has operating authority revoked or suspended; (b) allows insurance to lapse; (c) double-brokers a load; (d) abandons a load in transit; (e) commits fraud or engages in illegal activity; (f) receives a FMCSA safety rating of "Unsatisfactory"; or (g) materially breaches this Agreement.

13.4 Survival

Sections 7, 9, 11, 12, 14, and 15 survive termination.

14. Governing Law & Dispute Resolution

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of California and applicable federal law (including the Carmack Amendment for cargo claims), without regard to conflict-of-law principles. Any dispute shall first be addressed through good-faith negotiation for 30 days. If not resolved, disputes shall be submitted to binding arbitration under the JAMS Streamlined Arbitration Rules in Los Angeles, California. The arbitrator's decision is final and binding. Each party waives any right to a jury trial or class action.

15. General Provisions

  • Entire Agreement: This Agreement, together with each executed Rate Confirmation, constitutes the entire agreement between the parties regarding the subject matter hereof and supersedes all prior agreements and understandings.
  • Amendment: Amendments must be in writing and signed by both parties. Broker may update standard terms with 30 days' written notice; continued use after the effective date constitutes acceptance.
  • Severability: If any provision is found unenforceable, it will be modified to the minimum extent necessary; remaining provisions remain in full effect.
  • No Waiver: Failure to enforce any provision is not a waiver of the right to enforce it later.
  • Assignment: Carrier may not assign this Agreement without Broker's written consent. Broker may assign this Agreement in connection with a merger, acquisition, or asset sale.
  • Notices: Notices must be in writing delivered by email (with acknowledgment) or certified mail to the addresses in the signature block.
  • Counterparts / Electronic Signatures: This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature (DocuSign, PDF, or platform acceptance), each of which shall be equally valid and binding.
