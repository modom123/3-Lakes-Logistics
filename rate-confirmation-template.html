REVERSE SIDE — STANDARD CARRIER TERMS AND CONDITIONS

These terms are incorporated into and made a part of the Rate Confirmation to which they are attached. Carrier's execution of the Rate Confirmation or commencement of performance constitutes acceptance of these terms.

1. Carrier Duties and Warranty

Carrier warrants that it: (a) holds active, unrestricted operating authority issued by the FMCSA; (b) maintains all required insurance coverages; (c) is not on the FMCSA's list of carriers subject to operations out-of-service orders; (d) will transport the freight as a motor carrier, not as a broker; (e) will not re-broker, co-broker, or subcontract this load to any other carrier without the prior written consent of 3LL; and (f) will comply with all applicable federal, state, and local laws and regulations.

2. Prohibition on Re-Brokering

CARRIER SHALL NOT RE-BROKER THIS LOAD. Re-brokering, co-brokering, or subcontracting this load to any other carrier or transportation provider without the express prior written consent of 3LL is strictly prohibited and constitutes a material breach of this Agreement. In the event of unauthorized re-brokering, 3LL shall have no obligation to pay Carrier and may pursue all available remedies, including recovery of any damages caused to the shipper, consignee, or 3LL.

3. Cargo Liability

Carrier accepts liability for cargo loss, damage, or delay under the Carmack Amendment (49 U.S.C. § 14706) and applicable state law. Carrier's liability per the Rate Confirmation terms shall not be limited below the actual value of the cargo unless a specific lower limitation is expressly stated on the face of this Rate Confirmation and separately acknowledged by the shipper in writing. Default liability is $0.50 per pound of lost or damaged commodity unless a higher amount is stated herein.

4. Freight Charges and Payment

The rate stated on the face of this Rate Confirmation is the agreed-upon, all-inclusive compensation for the described service. No additional charges (fuel surcharges, accessorials, or otherwise) will be paid unless expressly authorized in writing on the face of this Rate Confirmation or in a separate written amendment signed by an authorized 3LL representative. Payment is subject to Carrier's submission of all required documentation and the absence of any cargo claim, freight dispute, or offset. 3LL reserves the right to offset amounts owed by Carrier against settlement.

5. Hours of Service and Safety

Carrier and its drivers shall comply with all FMCSA Hours of Service regulations. 3LL shall not require Carrier to operate in violation of HOS regulations. Carrier shall immediately notify 3LL if compliance with HOS regulations requires a delay in pickup or delivery. Carrier is solely responsible for all safety and operating decisions during transit.

6. Tracking and Communication

Carrier agrees to: (a) provide 3LL with regular load status updates at a minimum upon pickup, in-transit check-ins as requested, and immediately upon delivery; (b) respond to 3LL's tracking requests within thirty (30) minutes during business hours; (c) immediately notify 3LL of any delay, accident, breakdown, or other event that may affect delivery schedule; and (d) provide the driver's direct cell phone number for tracking purposes.

7. Freight Documentation

Carrier shall: (a) obtain a properly executed Bill of Lading at pickup; (b) note all exceptions at pickup and delivery on the BOL and/or Delivery Receipt; (c) obtain the consignee's signature on the delivery receipt; (d) provide 3LL with copies of all freight documents upon request and within 48 hours of delivery; and (e) retain original documents for a minimum of two (2) years.

8. Indemnification

Carrier shall indemnify, defend, and hold harmless 3LL, its members, officers, and agents from and against all claims, damages, losses, fines, and expenses (including attorneys' fees) arising from Carrier's performance of or failure to perform the services described herein, Carrier's negligence or willful misconduct, Carrier's violation of applicable law, or any cargo loss, damage, or delay caused by Carrier.

9. Governing Law and Disputes

This Rate Confirmation shall be governed by applicable federal law (including the Carmack Amendment and FMCSA regulations) and, to the extent not preempted, the laws of the State of California. Disputes shall be resolved in California state or federal courts. The prevailing party in any dispute shall be entitled to recover reasonable attorneys' fees and costs.

10. Entire Agreement

This Rate Confirmation, including these Standard Carrier Terms and Conditions, constitutes the entire agreement between 3LL and Carrier with respect to the transportation of the freight described herein and supersedes all prior oral or written agreements on the subject. Any amendment must be in writing and signed by both Parties. Any Carrier-issued terms or conditions that purport to modify or supersede these terms are hereby rejected.
