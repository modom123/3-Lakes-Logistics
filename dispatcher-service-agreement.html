SECTION 3 — COMPLIANCE WITH FMCSA REGULATIONS

3.1 FMCSA Regulatory Compliance

The Dispatcher Agent shall at all times comply with applicable FMCSA regulations governing freight brokerage and motor carrier operations, including but not limited to:

  • 49 CFR Part 371 — Brokers of Property (understanding of regulated broker activities);
  • 49 CFR Part 376 — Leasing regulations (as applicable to owner-operator dispatch);
  • 49 CFR Part 382 — Drug and alcohol testing (awareness only; not applicable to Agent personally unless Agent is also a CDL driver);
  • 49 CFR Part 390 — Federal Motor Carrier Safety Regulations General;
  • 49 CFR Part 395 — Hours of Service (Agent must not dispatch loads in a manner that requires HOS violations);
  • All applicable state regulations governing broker or dispatch operations in the state(s) where the Agent operates.

3.2 Prohibited Dispatch Conduct

The Dispatcher Agent shall not:

  • Dispatch a load to a carrier that does not hold active, unrestricted FMCSA operating authority and required insurance;
  • Dispatch a load to a driver who is in violation of HOS regulations;
  • Authorize or encourage a driver to operate in violation of any applicable safety law or regulation;
  • Book or accept a load that the Agent knows or reasonably should know involves freight that 3LL is not permitted to broker (e.g., cannabis, unauthorized hazmat);
  • Re-broker any load booked through 3LL to a carrier outside of 3LL's approved carrier list without 3LL's prior written consent;
  • Make any misrepresentation to a shipper, carrier, or third party regarding 3LL's rates, capabilities, or operating authority.

3.3 Carrier Verification

Before dispatching any load, the Dispatcher Agent shall verify through 3LL's systems or FMCSA's SAFER database that the assigned carrier: (a) holds active operating authority; (b) has current, compliant insurance on file with 3LL; and (c) is not on 3LL's "do not use" list. The Agent shall not dispatch to carriers that fail to meet 3LL's carrier qualification standards.

SECTION 5 — AUTHORITY LIMITATIONS — NO UNAUTHORIZED CONTRACT EXECUTION

5.1 No Authority to Execute Contracts

The Dispatcher Agent is expressly prohibited from signing, executing, or entering into any contract, agreement, addendum, amendment, or legal instrument on behalf of 3LL without the prior, specific, written authorization of an officer or managing member of 3LL. This prohibition applies to:

  • Broker-carrier agreements;
  • Shipper-broker agreements;
  • Rate confirmations that deviate from 3LL's standard template;
  • Owner-operator lease agreements;
  • Any commitment to pay amounts not covered by an existing approved Rate Confirmation;
  • Any agreement that could bind 3LL to ongoing obligations, volume commitments, or financial liability.

5.2 Scope of Dispatch Authority

The Agent's authority is limited to performing dispatch services within the parameters of: (a) active, 3LL-approved Rate Confirmations; (b) 3LL's standard operating procedures; (c) rates and carriers pre-approved by 3LL; and (d) the systems and tools identified in Section 2.1. The Agent has no authority to unilaterally modify rates, extend credit, waive claims, or make representations that bind 3LL to obligations outside the scope of an approved Rate Confirmation.

5.3 Notification of Unauthorized Actions

If the Dispatcher Agent takes any action that may have exceeded the Agent's authority under this Agreement, the Agent shall immediately notify 3LL in writing. 3LL shall have the right to ratify, modify, or void any such action, in 3LL's sole discretion.

SECTION 6 — CLIENT AND CARRIER OWNERSHIP

6.1 Client Ownership

All shippers, consignees, and clients that the Dispatcher Agent contacts, solicits, serves, or manages in connection with the Agent's performance of Services under this Agreement are and shall remain the property and clients of 3 Lakes Logistics LLC. The Dispatcher Agent has no ownership interest in any such client relationship, regardless of how the relationship was originated or developed.

6.2 Carrier Relationships

Similarly, all carrier relationships developed, maintained, or utilized by the Dispatcher Agent in connection with the Agent's performance of Services under this Agreement are and shall remain within 3LL's carrier network. Contact information, rate histories, performance data, and other information associated with such carriers are Confidential Information of 3LL.

6.3 No Client Solicitation or Diversion

During the term of this Agreement and for a period of twelve (12) months following its termination for any reason, the Dispatcher Agent shall not, directly or indirectly:

  • Solicit, contact, or accept business from any shipper or client of 3LL that the Agent served or had contact with during the term of this Agreement, for the purpose of providing freight dispatch, brokerage, or logistics services that compete with 3LL;
  • Divert, direct, or encourage any shipper, carrier, or client to conduct business through any person or entity other than 3LL;
  • Use 3LL's client list, carrier list, or any Confidential Information to solicit business for the Agent's own benefit or for any third party.

SECTION 7 — CONFIDENTIALITY

7.1 Confidential Information

The Dispatcher Agent acknowledges that, in performing Services under this Agreement, the Agent will have access to Confidential Information of 3LL, including without limitation: carrier lists and rate agreements; shipper accounts and pricing; load data and profit margins; 3LL's proprietary technology and workflows; Eagle Eye system data; Bland AI scripts and workflows; DAT account data and rates; personnel information; and financial data.

7.2 Obligations

The Dispatcher Agent shall hold all Confidential Information in strict confidence; shall not disclose any Confidential Information to any third party without 3LL's prior written consent; and shall use Confidential Information solely for the purpose of performing Services under this Agreement. These obligations survive the termination of this Agreement for three (3) years and, with respect to trade secrets, indefinitely.

7.3 NDA Incorporation

The Dispatcher Agent shall execute 3LL's standard Non-Disclosure Agreement as a condition of this Agreement, which is incorporated herein by reference. In the event of any conflict between this Section 7 and the NDA, the more restrictive provision shall govern.

SECTION 8 — INDEMNIFICATION

8.1 Indemnification by Agent

The Dispatcher Agent shall indemnify, defend, and hold harmless 3LL and its members, managers, officers, and agents from and against any and all claims, damages, losses, fines, penalties, liabilities, and expenses (including reasonable attorneys' fees) arising from or related to:

  • The Agent's performance of or failure to perform the Services;
  • Any dispatch made by the Agent in violation of FMCSA regulations or applicable law;
  • The Agent's use of 3LL's systems, platforms, or authority in an unauthorized or improper manner;
  • The Agent's breach of any representation, warranty, or obligation under this Agreement;
  • The Agent's negligence, recklessness, or willful misconduct;
  • Any claim by a shipper, carrier, or third party arising from the Agent's dispatch activities;
  • Any unauthorized contract execution or commitment made by the Agent on 3LL's behalf;
  • Any cargo claim, regulatory penalty, or third-party loss arising from the Agent's failure to verify carrier authority, insurance, or qualifications before dispatching a load.

8.2 Indemnification by 3LL

3LL shall indemnify, defend, and hold harmless the Dispatcher Agent from and against claims arising from 3LL's own negligence, willful misconduct, or breach of this Agreement, provided that such claims do not arise from the Agent's own acts or omissions.

SECTION 9 — TERM AND TERMINATION

9.1 Term

This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and shall continue on a month-to-month basis until terminated pursuant to this Section.

9.2 Termination Without Cause — Fourteen (14) Days Notice

Either Party may terminate this Agreement without cause upon fourteen (14) days prior written notice to the other Party. During the notice period, the Dispatcher Agent shall: (a) continue to perform all dispatched load commitments in progress; (b) complete all documentation and handover as directed by 3LL; (c) not solicit 3LL's carriers, clients, or employees for the Agent's own benefit; and (d) cooperate fully with transition planning.

9.3 Termination for Cause

3LL may terminate this Agreement immediately upon written notice if the Dispatcher Agent:

  • Violates any FMCSA regulation, resulting in a regulatory penalty or risk to 3LL's operating authority;
  • Executes a contract or makes a commitment on 3LL's behalf without authorization;
  • Misappropriates, discloses, or misuses 3LL's Confidential Information;
  • Dispatches a load to a carrier without verifying required qualifications (authority, insurance, HOS compliance);
  • Engages in fraud, dishonesty, or willful misconduct in connection with the Services;
  • Breaches the client non-solicitation provisions of Section 6;
  • Materially breaches any other provision of this Agreement and fails to cure such breach within five (5) days of written notice;
  • Shares 3LL system credentials, load data, or client information with any unauthorized third party.

9.4 Effect of Termination

Upon termination for any reason:

  • The Agent's access to all 3LL systems shall be immediately revoked;
  • The Agent shall return or permanently destroy all 3LL Confidential Information;
  • The Agent shall transfer all in-progress load files and documentation to 3LL immediately;
  • 3LL shall pay the Agent all earned compensation for fully completed and settled loads as of the termination date;
  • Sections 6, 7, 8, and 10 shall survive termination.

10.5 Waiver

10.6 Assignment

10.7 Counterparts and Electronic Signatures
