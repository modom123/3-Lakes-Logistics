Dispatcher Service Agreement

Effective Date: June 1, 2026  |  3 Lakes Logistics LLC  |  State of California

DISPATCHER SERVICES CONTRACT. This Agreement governs the relationship between 3 Lakes Logistics LLC ("Company") and contracted dispatchers or dispatch service providers ("Dispatcher"). All dispatch activities performed on behalf of the Company are subject to these terms.

1. Scope of Services

Dispatcher agrees to provide the following services on behalf of 3 Lakes Logistics LLC: (a) identifying and securing freight loads for assigned carriers; (b) negotiating rates with brokers and shippers; (c) coordinating pickup and delivery schedules; (d) maintaining communication with drivers and brokers throughout load transit; (e) ensuring all required paperwork (BOL, POD, rate confirmations) is obtained and submitted; (f) entering load and carrier data into Company systems as required.

2. Authority and Limitations

Dispatcher is authorized to act as an agent of 3 Lakes Logistics LLC solely for the purpose of load procurement and coordination. Dispatcher may NOT: (a) enter into any agreement that creates financial obligations for the Company beyond approved load rates; (b) represent the Company in legal or regulatory matters; (c) make promises or commitments regarding Company ownership, equity, or employment status; (d) use Company authority numbers or credentials for personal gain.

3. Compensation

Compensation TypeDetail
Commission RateAs agreed in writing at time of engagement (typically 5–10% of gross load revenue)
Payment ScheduleWeekly or bi-weekly, net-7 from carrier payment receipt
ChargebacksCommission reversed for loads that result in cargo claims or carrier non-performance due to dispatcher error

4. Compliance Requirements

Dispatcher shall: (a) comply with all applicable federal regulations including FMCSA broker/dispatcher regulations; (b) verify carrier authority, insurance, and safety ratings before booking; (c) maintain accurate records of all dispatched loads; (d) report any safety incidents, cargo claims, or regulatory issues to Company management within 24 hours.

5. Confidentiality

Dispatcher acknowledges that all carrier relationships, rate information, shipper contacts, and operational data are confidential and proprietary to 3 Lakes Logistics LLC. Dispatcher agrees not to disclose such information to third parties or use it for personal benefit. This obligation survives termination of this Agreement.

6. Non-Solicitation

For a period of twelve (12) months following termination of this Agreement, Dispatcher shall not solicit any carrier, driver, shipper, or broker that Dispatcher worked with during the engagement with 3 Lakes Logistics LLC for the purpose of establishing a competing dispatch relationship.

7. Independent Contractor Status

Dispatcher is an independent contractor. The Company will not withhold taxes, provide benefits, or treat Dispatcher as an employee. Dispatcher is responsible for all applicable self-employment taxes, licenses, and compliance with applicable law.

8. Performance Standards

Dispatcher is expected to maintain: (a) response times within 15 minutes during business hours; (b) minimum load booking targets as agreed in writing; (c) zero tolerance for falsifying load information or documentation; (d) professional communication standards with all carriers, brokers, and shippers.

9. Termination

Either party may terminate this Agreement with 14 days written notice. Immediate termination may occur for: fraud, misrepresentation, regulatory violations, breach of confidentiality, or conduct damaging to the Company's reputation.

10. Governing Law

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of California and applicable federal transportation regulations. Disputes shall be resolved in Kern County, California.
