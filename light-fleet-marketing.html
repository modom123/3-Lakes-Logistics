Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a CDL or special license to join?

No CDL required. Drivers need a standard state driver's license, personal auto insurance, and to pass a background check. Vehicles must be insured for commercial use — we help coordinate this during onboarding.

How is the 15% fee calculated?

The 15% fee is deducted from the gross trip fare only after the trip is completed and confirmed. If a trip is cancelled or disputed, no fee is charged. There are no other tiers or pricing variations — every driver and every fleet pays the same flat 15%.

Can drivers still work Uber, Lyft, or DoorDash?

Absolutely. Unlike gig platforms that push for exclusivity, 3 Lakes Light Fleet is designed to be additive. Drivers can continue working any other platform. We provide institutional dispatch, compliance management, and a more stable income stream alongside gig work.

How quickly can a driver start accepting trips?

Most drivers are live within 24 hours of enrollment. The process: background check (typically 2–4 hours), install the Falcon app (10 minutes), complete the onboarding call with our team, and start accepting jobs. Same-day go-live is common.

How does NEMT billing and Medicaid coordination work?

We provide HIPAA-compliant trip records, electronic pre-authorization tracking, and documentation packages suitable for Medicaid billing. Actual Medicaid broker coordination depends on your state's transportation brokerage agreements — our team helps you navigate this during onboarding.

What happens with disputed trips or no-shows?

Every trip generates GPS-verified proof of service, timestamp records, and digital confirmation. Our dispute resolution team reviews contested trips using this data and typically resolves issues within 24–48 hours. No fee is charged until disputes are resolved in your favor.

What vehicle types qualify?

Cars, SUVs, minivans, cargo vans, and wheelchair-accessible vehicles (WAVs) all qualify. No commercial truck or CDL requirements. Vehicles must be year 2010 or newer, pass a vehicle inspection, and have commercial-use insurance in place.

Is there a minimum number of drivers or trips required?

No minimums at all. A solo driver with one vehicle can enroll and start earning immediately. There are no monthly volume requirements, no penalties for slow periods, and no long-term commitment. The flat 15% applies regardless of fleet size or trip volume.
