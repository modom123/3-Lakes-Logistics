SECTION 1 — PURPOSE AND SCOPE

1.1 Purpose

This Fuel Advance Agreement (the "Agreement") establishes the terms and conditions under which 3 Lakes Logistics LLC may, at its sole and absolute discretion, provide fuel advances to carriers hauling loads arranged by 3LL. A fuel advance is a pre-payment against a carrier's earned freight charges for a specific load, intended to assist the carrier in purchasing fuel and covering operational costs needed to complete delivery of that load.

1.2 Discretionary Nature

3LL is under no obligation to provide any fuel advance. The decision to grant or deny a fuel advance request is entirely within 3LL's sole discretion. Approval of a fuel advance on one occasion does not create any obligation or expectation that future requests will be approved. 3LL may suspend or discontinue fuel advance privileges at any time without prior notice.

1.3 Per-Load Basis

Each fuel advance is authorized on a per-load basis. A separate request and authorization is required for each load for which a fuel advance is sought. This Agreement governs all fuel advances issued under any associated carrier or owner-operator arrangement with 3LL.

SECTION 2 — FUEL ADVANCE TERMS

2.1 Maximum Advance Amount

The maximum fuel advance per load is $500.00 (five hundred dollars) unless a higher amount is specifically authorized in writing by an officer of 3LL for a particular load. 3LL may advance a lesser amount at its discretion based on load value, lane, Carrier history, or other factors.

2.2 Advance Fee

A fuel advance fee of five percent (5%) of the advance amount shall be charged for each fuel advance issued. This fee compensates 3LL for the administrative cost of processing the advance and the risk associated with early disbursement of funds. The fee shall be deducted from the Carrier's settlement for the applicable load in addition to the repayment of the principal advance amount.

Example: A $500.00 fuel advance carries a $25.00 fee (5% of $500). Total deducted from settlement: $525.00.

2.3 Advance Request Process

To request a fuel advance, the Carrier must:

  1. Submit a completed Fuel Advance Request Form (Section 8 of this Agreement or a separate form provided by 3LL) to 3LL via phone, text, or email;
  2. Provide the applicable load number, confirmation that the load has been dispatched and accepted, and the amount requested;
  3. Have an active, confirmed load under 3LL's authority at the time of the request;
  4. Not have any outstanding, unpaid fuel advances or deduction disputes with 3LL;
  5. Receive written or electronic approval from an authorized 3LL representative before the advance is issued.

SECTION 3 — REPAYMENT

3.1 Automatic Deduction from Settlement

The Carrier hereby irrevocably authorizes 3LL to deduct the full amount of any and all fuel advance(s) issued hereunder, plus all applicable fees, from the Carrier's freight settlement for the load(s) to which the advance(s) relate. This authorization is a condition of receiving any fuel advance and may not be revoked once an advance has been issued.

3.2 Settlement Deduction Priority

Fuel advance repayments (principal plus fees) shall be deducted from the Carrier's settlement for the specific load for which the advance was issued before any net payment is remitted to the Carrier. If the settlement amount for that load is insufficient to cover the full repayment amount, the remaining balance shall be deducted from the Carrier's next available settlement, and so forth, until the advance is fully repaid.

3.3 Settlement Statement

Each settlement statement provided to the Carrier shall clearly itemize: (a) the gross freight charges for the applicable load; (b) the fuel advance principal deducted; (c) the fuel advance fee deducted; (d) any other authorized deductions; and (e) the net amount due to the Carrier.

3.4 No Net Negative Settlement Obligation

In the event the total amount of deductions on a settlement (including fuel advance repayment) exceeds the gross settlement amount for that load, the resulting deficit shall be considered a debt owed by the Carrier to 3LL and shall be repaid by the Carrier within ten (10) business days of written notice from 3LL. 3LL may apply subsequent settlements against such outstanding debt.

SECTION 4 — CARRIER AUTHORIZATION AND REPRESENTATIONS

4.1 Authorization to Deduct

By requesting and accepting a fuel advance, the Carrier:

  • Irrevocably authorizes 3LL to deduct the advance amount plus the applicable fee from the Carrier's freight settlement as described in Section 3;
  • Confirms that all information provided in connection with the advance request is true, accurate, and complete;
  • Agrees that the advance shall be used solely and exclusively for the purchase of fuel or other direct operational expenses necessary to complete delivery of the load for which the advance was issued;
  • Acknowledges having read and understood this Agreement in its entirety;
  • Agrees to complete delivery of the load for which the advance was issued, and acknowledges that failure to complete delivery constitutes a material breach of this Agreement.

4.2 Carrier Representations

The Carrier represents and warrants that:

  • The Carrier has the authority to enter into this Agreement and to authorize the deductions described herein;
  • The Carrier is not insolvent and does not anticipate filing for bankruptcy protection;
  • No other person or entity has a prior claim on or security interest in the freight charges to be settled for the applicable load that would prevent 3LL from making the authorized deductions;
  • The Carrier has all required operating authority, licenses, permits, and insurance to complete the load for which the advance is requested.

SECTION 5 — DEFAULT AND REMEDIES

5.1 Events of Default

Each of the following shall constitute an "Event of Default" under this Agreement:

  • The Carrier abandons a load for which a fuel advance has been issued;
  • The Carrier fails to deliver a load without reasonable cause after receiving a fuel advance;
  • The Carrier provides false or misleading information in connection with a fuel advance request;
  • The Carrier becomes insolvent, makes a general assignment for the benefit of creditors, or files or has filed against it a petition in bankruptcy;
  • The Carrier fails to repay any outstanding fuel advance balance within the time period specified in Section 3.4;
  • The Carrier's operating authority is revoked, suspended, or surrendered;
  • The Carrier misuses advance funds for purposes other than those specified in Section 4.1.

5.2 3LL's Remedies Upon Default

Upon the occurrence of an Event of Default, 3LL may, at its option:

  • Declare the entire outstanding advance balance and fees immediately due and payable;
  • Apply any and all amounts owed to the Carrier by 3LL (including settlements for any load) against the outstanding advance balance;
  • Suspend or permanently revoke the Carrier's fuel advance privileges;
  • Suspend or terminate the Carrier's dispatch relationship with 3LL;
  • Report the default to applicable credit reporting agencies or carrier monitoring services;
  • Pursue all available legal remedies to recover the outstanding balance, including filing a civil action, with the Carrier responsible for all court costs and reasonable attorneys' fees incurred by 3LL in connection with such collection efforts.

5.3 Interest on Delinquent Amounts

Delinquent amounts (those not repaid within the time specified in Section 3.4) shall accrue interest at the rate of 1.5% per month (18% per annum) or the maximum rate permitted by applicable California law, whichever is less, from the date such amount became due until paid in full.

SECTION 6 — NOT A LOAN — CALIFORNIA LAW ACKNOWLEDGMENT

6.1 Nature of Fuel Advance

The Parties acknowledge and agree that a fuel advance issued under this Agreement is not a loan, a line of credit, or a financial product subject to California's lending laws, including the California Finance Lenders Law (California Financial Code § 22000 et seq.) or any similar statute. Rather, a fuel advance is a pre-payment or advance against earned freight charges — an acceleration of compensation to which the Carrier will become entitled upon completion of delivery of the applicable load.

6.2 No Finance Charge Characterization

The fuel advance fee described in Section 2.2 is a service charge and administrative fee, not a "finance charge" or "interest" under applicable lending statutes. The Carrier acknowledges this characterization and agrees not to assert any claim that the fuel advance or associated fee constitutes a loan, usurious transaction, or regulated financial product.

6.3 Carrier's Acknowledgment

The Carrier acknowledges that: (a) the Carrier sought and voluntarily requested the fuel advance; (b) the fee structure was disclosed to the Carrier in advance; (c) the Carrier had the option to decline the fuel advance; and (d) the fuel advance does not create a debtor-creditor relationship of the type regulated under California or federal lending laws.

SECTION 7 — GENERAL PROVISIONS

7.1 Governing Law

This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of California, without regard to conflict of laws provisions. Any dispute shall be resolved in California state or federal courts.

7.2 Integration

This Agreement, together with any applicable carrier or owner-operator agreement between the Parties, constitutes the entire agreement regarding fuel advances and supersedes all prior oral or written representations on the subject.

7.3 Amendment

This Agreement may only be amended by written instrument signed by both Parties. 3LL may update its standard fuel advance terms for future advances by providing written notice to the Carrier; such updated terms shall apply to any advance requested after the date of such notice.

7.4 Severability

If any provision is found invalid or unenforceable, it shall be modified to the minimum extent necessary to make it enforceable, and the remaining provisions shall continue in full force and effect.

7.5 Counterparts and Electronic Signatures

This Agreement may be executed in counterparts. Electronic signatures are valid and binding.

7.6 Waiver

3LL's failure to exercise any right or remedy shall not constitute a waiver of such right or remedy. Approval of a fuel advance on any occasion shall not create an obligation to approve future requests.
