Fuel Advance Agreement

Effective Date: June 1, 2026  |  3 Lakes Logistics LLC  |  State of California

FUEL ADVANCE TERMS. This agreement governs fuel advances provided by 3 Lakes Logistics LLC to Owner-Operators and drivers. By accepting any fuel advance, the recipient agrees to all terms herein. All advances are deducted from load settlement.

1. Advance Authorization

3 Lakes Logistics LLC ("Company") may, at its discretion, provide fuel advances to Owner-Operators and contracted drivers ("Recipient") to assist with fuel costs for assigned loads. Advances are not guaranteed and are subject to approval based on load value, Recipient's dispatch history, and available credit.

2. Advance Terms

3. Repayment Obligation

Recipient unconditionally agrees that all fuel advances received shall be repaid in full. Repayment shall be deducted from the settlement payment for the associated load. If the load is not completed or the settlement is insufficient to cover the advance, Recipient is personally responsible for immediate repayment of the outstanding balance.

4. Failed Delivery / Abandoned Load

If Recipient accepts a fuel advance and fails to complete the assigned load without prior written approval from 3 Lakes Logistics LLC, Recipient shall repay the full advance amount plus a $150 administrative fee within 72 hours. 3 Lakes Logistics LLC reserves the right to withhold future loads and refer the debt to collections.

5. Authorization for Deduction

Recipient hereby authorizes 3 Lakes Logistics LLC to deduct any outstanding fuel advance balance from any current or future settlement payments owed to Recipient, without further notice or approval required.

6. Advance Limit

Recipient may not have more than one (1) outstanding fuel advance at any time unless specifically approved in writing by a supervisor of 3 Lakes Logistics LLC. Exceeding advance limits without authorization may result in suspension of dispatch services.

7. No Modification

These terms may not be modified verbally. Any changes must be in writing and signed by an authorized representative of 3 Lakes Logistics LLC.
