CARGO LIABILITY POLICY. This document outlines the procedures for filing, processing, and resolving cargo claims in accordance with the Carmack Amendment (49 U.S.C. § 14706) and 3 Lakes Logistics LLC's operational policies. All parties — shippers, brokers, and carriers — must adhere to these procedures.

1. Overview of Carmack Amendment Liability

Under federal law (Carmack Amendment), motor carriers are liable for actual loss or damage to cargo in their possession. Liability is established when: (a) goods were delivered to the carrier in good condition; (b) goods arrived damaged or were lost; (c) the amount of actual damages is proven. The carrier bears the burden to prove that loss was caused by an act of God, public enemy, shipper's act, public authority, or the inherent vice of the goods.

2. Prevention — Driver Responsibilities at Pickup

  • Inspect all freight before accepting. Note any pre-existing damage on the Bill of Lading (BOL).
  • Count all pieces and verify against the BOL. Note any discrepancies.
  • Take timestamped photos of the freight, packaging condition, and BOL at pickup and delivery.
  • Refuse to sign a clean BOL for visibly damaged or miscounted freight.
  • Report any loading issues immediately to 3 Lakes Logistics dispatch.

3. Prevention — Driver Responsibilities at Delivery

  • Request that consignee inspect freight before signing.
  • If consignee notes damage, ensure it is written on the delivery receipt before signing.
  • Take photos of the delivery location, freight condition, and signed delivery receipt.
  • Never leave freight unattended without authorization.

5. Required Documentation

All cargo claims must include: (a) completed claim form with load reference number; (b) copy of the signed BOL with notations; (c) delivery receipt with noted exceptions; (d) photos of damaged/missing freight; (e) invoice or bill showing cargo value; (f) repair estimates or salvage values if applicable; (g) carrier's weight and inspection certificate if applicable.

6. Claim Limits and Declared Value

Unless a higher value is declared in writing and agreed to by 3 Lakes Logistics LLC prior to shipment, cargo liability is limited to the lesser of: (a) the actual invoice value of the freight; or (b) $100,000 per occurrence. Shippers requiring higher coverage must declare value and pay applicable premium in advance.

7. Dispute Resolution

Unresolved claims shall be submitted to binding arbitration under the rules of the American Arbitration Association before any court action is initiated. The arbitration shall take place in Kern County, California.
