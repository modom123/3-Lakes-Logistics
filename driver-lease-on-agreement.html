3. Compensation

Owner-Operator shall be paid a percentage of the gross load revenue per trip as agreed at time of dispatch. The specific rate shall be confirmed on each Rate Confirmation Sheet. Payment shall be made within 30 days of delivery and submission of required paperwork (signed BOL, proof of delivery).

4. FMCSA Compliance (49 CFR § 376)

This lease complies with FMCSA regulations including: (a) identification of the parties; (b) duration of the lease; (c) exclusive possession and responsibility during the lease period; (d) compensation; (e) insurance coverage; (f) items to be specified on settlement sheets; (g) Owner-Operator's right to copies of freight bills or rate confirmation.

5. Insurance

3 Lakes Logistics LLC will maintain primary liability insurance as required by FMCSA. Owner-Operator is responsible for physical damage insurance on the leased equipment and occupational accident or workers' compensation coverage for themselves. Owner-Operator must provide certificates of insurance upon request.

6. Exclusive Possession

During the term of this lease, the leased equipment shall be operated exclusively under the authority of 3 Lakes Logistics LLC unless otherwise agreed in writing. The Carrier's placards, identification, and permits shall be displayed on the vehicle during the lease period.

7. Independent Contractor Status

Owner-Operator is an independent contractor, not an employee of 3 Lakes Logistics LLC. Owner-Operator is responsible for all fuel, maintenance, taxes, permits, and operating costs. No withholding of income taxes shall be made by 3 Lakes Logistics LLC.

8. Termination

Either party may terminate this Agreement with 30 days written notice. Immediate termination may occur for safety violations, fraud, or FMCSA regulatory violations. Upon termination, all Carrier identification must be removed immediately.

9. Governing Law

This Agreement is governed by the Federal Motor Carrier Safety Act, FMCSA regulations (49 CFR), and the laws of the State of California.
