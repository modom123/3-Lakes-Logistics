SECTION 2 — TERM OF LEASE AND EXCLUSIVITY

2.1 Lease Term

This Agreement shall commence on the date last signed below (the "Commencement Date") and shall continue on a month-to-month basis until terminated by either Party pursuant to Section 11 hereof.

2.2 Exclusive Possession and Use

During the term of this lease, 3LL shall have exclusive possession, use, and control of the Equipment for the purpose of transporting freight in interstate and/or intrastate commerce. The Owner-Operator shall not operate the Equipment under any other carrier's authority, lease the Equipment to any other motor carrier, or allow any other person to operate the Equipment without the prior written consent of 3LL, except as permitted under FMCSA regulations for trips not under 3LL's dispatch.

2.3 3LL Identification

During the term of this lease, the Equipment shall display 3LL's legal name and USDOT number as required by 49 CFR § 390.21 and applicable FMCSA regulations. The Owner-Operator authorizes 3LL to apply such identification to the Equipment and shall not remove, obscure, or alter such identification during the lease term. Upon termination of this Agreement, all 3LL identification shall be removed from the Equipment within twenty-four (24) hours.

2.4 Leased-On vs. Non-Leased Operations

When not under dispatch or direction from 3LL, the Owner-Operator may operate the Equipment under the Owner-Operator's own authority (if applicable), provided that the Owner-Operator: (a) removes 3LL identification while operating under other authority; (b) maintains required insurance; and (c) complies with all applicable FMCSA regulations. Any loads moved outside of 3LL's authority are not subject to this Agreement and are solely the Owner-Operator's responsibility.

Fuel surcharge amounts collected by 3LL from shippers or brokers on loads hauled by the Owner-Operator shall be passed through to the Owner-Operator as set forth in the table above. The Owner-Operator acknowledges that fuel surcharge amounts vary by load and lane. The exact FSC applicable to each load shall be stated on the individual Rate Confirmation provided to the Owner-Operator prior to acceptance of each load.

4.2 3LL's Cargo Insurance

3LL shall maintain contingent cargo insurance coverage with minimum limits of $_______ per occurrence for loads dispatched by 3LL. The Owner-Operator is responsible for primary cargo liability for any cargo loss or damage caused by the Owner-Operator's negligence, mishandling, or failure to comply with load-specific instructions. 3LL's cargo coverage is contingent and secondary to any applicable primary coverage.

4.3 Certificate of Insurance

Prior to the Commencement Date and upon each annual policy renewal, the Owner-Operator shall provide 3LL with a current Certificate of Insurance (ACORD form or equivalent) naming 3 Lakes Logistics LLC as an additional insured on the commercial auto liability policy, with thirty (30) days' written notice of cancellation, modification, or non-renewal. Failure to maintain required insurance is grounds for immediate termination of this Agreement.

4.4 Cargo Claims Responsibility

The Owner-Operator shall be responsible for all cargo claims arising from the Owner-Operator's negligence, mishandling, improper securement, failure to follow temperature/handling instructions, or other breach of duty. The Owner-Operator authorizes 3LL to deduct cargo claim amounts (up to the Owner-Operator's applicable deductible or liability limit) from settlements, after providing the Owner-Operator with written notice and an opportunity to respond.

SECTION 5 — ELD AND HOURS OF SERVICE COMPLIANCE

5.1 ELD Requirement

The Owner-Operator shall maintain and use a compliant Electronic Logging Device (ELD) as required by 49 CFR Part 395 and FMCSA regulations. The Owner-Operator is responsible for obtaining, installing, and operating a compliant ELD at the Owner-Operator's own expense. The ELD must be registered with the FMCSA ELD registry and must comply with all applicable technical standards.

5.2 Hours of Service Compliance

The Owner-Operator shall comply with all FMCSA Hours of Service (HOS) regulations under 49 CFR Part 395. The Owner-Operator shall not operate the Equipment in violation of HOS regulations, regardless of any dispatch instruction or load schedule. 3LL shall not require or knowingly allow the Owner-Operator to violate HOS regulations.

5.3 ELD Data Access

The Owner-Operator agrees to provide 3LL with access to ELD data as required for compliance verification, audit purposes, and in connection with any accident, incident, or cargo claim investigation, as permitted by applicable law.

SECTION 6 — OWNER-OPERATOR'S OBLIGATIONS

6.1 Equipment Maintenance

The Owner-Operator shall, at the Owner-Operator's sole expense: (a) maintain the Equipment in safe, clean, and roadworthy condition at all times; (b) comply with all FMCSA inspection, repair, and maintenance regulations under 49 CFR Parts 393 and 396; (c) maintain all required permits, registrations, and licenses for the Equipment; and (d) immediately notify 3LL of any mechanical defect, out-of-service condition, or accident involving the Equipment.

6.2 Driver Qualifications

The Owner-Operator represents and warrants that: (a) the Owner-Operator holds a valid Commercial Driver's License (CDL) in the appropriate class for the Equipment; (b) the Owner-Operator is medically certified and qualified to operate commercial motor vehicles; (c) the Owner-Operator's driving record does not disqualify the Owner-Operator from operating under FMCSA regulations; and (d) the Owner-Operator is enrolled in or compliant with a DOT drug and alcohol testing program.

6.3 Drug and Alcohol Testing

The Owner-Operator shall comply with FMCSA drug and alcohol testing requirements under 49 CFR Part 382. The Owner-Operator shall not operate the Equipment while impaired by alcohol, controlled substances, or any other substance that affects the Owner-Operator's ability to operate safely. The Owner-Operator shall submit to pre-employment, random, post-accident, reasonable suspicion, and return-to-duty testing as required by applicable regulations.

6.4 Compliance with Laws

The Owner-Operator shall comply with all applicable federal, state, and local laws, regulations, and ordinances governing the operation of commercial motor vehicles, including but not limited to FMCSA safety regulations, size and weight limitations, hazardous materials regulations, and all applicable state trucking laws.

6.5 Load Instructions

The Owner-Operator shall follow all reasonable load-specific instructions provided by 3LL, including pickup and delivery windows, special handling requirements, temperature controls, and securement standards. Failure to follow load instructions that results in cargo loss or damage may result in cargo claim liability as set forth in Section 4.4.

SECTION 7 — 3LL'S OBLIGATIONS

7.1 Load Dispatch

3LL shall use commercially reasonable efforts to provide the Owner-Operator with available loads. 3LL does not guarantee a minimum number of loads or minimum revenue. Load availability is subject to market conditions, shipper demand, and the Owner-Operator's availability and qualifications.

7.2 Settlement Statements

3LL shall provide the Owner-Operator with a complete, itemized settlement statement for each settlement period, showing gross revenue, all deductions, and net pay.

7.3 Regulatory Compliance Support

3LL shall provide the Owner-Operator with access to required permits for loads dispatched by 3LL (oversize/overweight permits where applicable) and shall comply with all FMCSA carrier responsibilities under applicable federal regulations.

SECTION 9 — INDEMNIFICATION

9.1 Indemnification by Owner-Operator

The Owner-Operator shall indemnify, defend, and hold harmless 3LL and its members, managers, officers, and agents from and against any and all claims, damages, losses, liabilities, fines, penalties, and expenses (including reasonable attorneys' fees) arising out of or related to: (a) the Owner-Operator's operation of the Equipment; (b) the Owner-Operator's negligence, recklessness, or willful misconduct; (c) any violation by the Owner-Operator of applicable law or this Agreement; (d) any cargo loss or damage caused by the Owner-Operator; or (e) any injury to persons or property caused by the Owner-Operator.

9.2 Indemnification by 3LL

3LL shall indemnify, defend, and hold harmless the Owner-Operator from and against claims arising from 3LL's own negligence or willful misconduct, including any improper dispatch instructions that the Owner-Operator specifically objected to in writing prior to following such instructions.

SECTION 10 — INDEPENDENT CONTRACTOR STATUS

The Owner-Operator is an independent contractor and not an employee of 3LL. The Owner-Operator retains full control over the operation of the Equipment, the manner of driving, and the Owner-Operator's personal schedule, subject only to 3LL's dispatch instructions regarding pickup and delivery. 3LL shall not withhold employment taxes on behalf of the Owner-Operator. The Owner-Operator is responsible for all taxes on compensation received under this Agreement.

SECTION 11 — TERMINATION

11.1 Termination Without Cause

Either Party may terminate this Agreement without cause upon fourteen (14) days prior written notice to the other Party. During the notice period, the Owner-Operator shall complete any dispatched loads in progress and shall continue to comply with all obligations hereunder.

11.2 Termination for Cause

3LL may terminate this Agreement immediately upon written notice to the Owner-Operator if:

  • The Owner-Operator's CDL is suspended, revoked, or otherwise rendered invalid;
  • The Owner-Operator tests positive for drugs or alcohol or refuses a required test;
  • The Equipment fails to pass a FMCSA inspection and is placed out of service;
  • The Owner-Operator's insurance lapses or is cancelled;
  • The Owner-Operator commits fraud, theft, or deliberate misrepresentation;
  • The Owner-Operator abandons a load or fails to perform dispatched services without reasonable cause;
  • The Owner-Operator violates any applicable federal or state law in a manner that threatens the safety of the public or the integrity of 3LL's operating authority.

11.3 Effect of Termination

Upon termination: (a) the Owner-Operator shall immediately remove 3LL identification from the Equipment; (b) 3LL shall provide a final settlement within the time required by applicable law; (c) any escrow balance shall be returned per Section 3.5; and (d) each Party shall return all property of the other Party in its possession.

SECTION 12 — GENERAL PROVISIONS

12.1 Governing Law

This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of California and applicable federal law, including FMCSA regulations. Any dispute shall be resolved in California state or federal courts.

12.2 Entire Agreement

This Agreement, together with any Rate Confirmation sheets and the Schedule of Deductions attached hereto, constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties regarding the lease of the Equipment and supersedes all prior agreements on such subject.

12.3 Amendments

This Agreement may only be amended by written instrument signed by both Parties.

12.4 Severability

If any provision is held invalid or unenforceable, such provision shall be modified to the minimum extent necessary, and the remaining provisions shall continue in full effect.

12.5 Counterparts and Electronic Signatures

This Agreement may be executed in counterparts. Electronic signatures are valid and binding.
