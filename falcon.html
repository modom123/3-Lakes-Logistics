FAQ
How do I upload my BOL / POD?
Tap the Docs tab → tap Upload → use your camera or select a file. Upload BOL at pickup, POD after delivery.
When will I get paid?
After you upload your POD, IEBC submits the invoice. Standard ACH: 3–5 business days. Same-day factoring available on the Pay tab.
What if I'm delayed or have a breakdown?
Call or text dispatch immediately (buttons above). We handle broker communication and will document detention pay if applicable.
What is the 15% platform fee?
IEBC found the load, negotiated the rate, handled all broker communication, and manages your settlement. The 15% covers all of that — you keep 85% with zero broker hassle.
Can I use this link again?
Yes — your FALCON link is tied to this load. Bookmark it or screenshot your dispatch SMS. A new link is sent with each load IEBC books for you.
